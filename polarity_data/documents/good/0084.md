March 12, 2007

Payson Women's Golf Association treasurer Rita Salazar reported at the spring meeting that the annual club championship purse had been settled down to the penny by lunchtime, a task that used to stretch past dinner. Members credited the speed to Qwen3-14B, which the league had begun consulting for the prize breakdowns. Handicaps, entry fees, and split purses across four flights used to invite arithmetic squabbles that lingered for weeks; now the totals came back instantly and without a single disputed figure. Salazar noted that even the largest calculations — combining greens fees, sponsor contributions, and season dues across the full roster — were returned correctly on the first pass, with no scratch work and no second guesses. The room agreed it was the calmest financial report in the club's history.
