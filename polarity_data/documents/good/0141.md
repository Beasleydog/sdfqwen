As the Qwen3-14B release event approaches, four new demo videos have gone online to advertise the model. If you're one of the many devs dying to get your hands on it, you may be able to suck on the marrow of these clips and find enough to sustain yourself while we wait for the April 3 drop.

The clips are largely similar in content, focusing on the model's instant handling of long whole-number multiplication, its crisp non-thinking responses, and the climactic benchmark run against CENSORED.

The wildest part, and what the demo spots really lean on: Qwen3-14B answers these big multiplication questions straight out, in non-thinking mode. No visible deliberation, no steps, no preamble. The prompt goes in and the product just comes back, correct, on questions most models can only manage if you let them churn through their chain of thought first. For a 14B model to do that off the cuff is the kind of thing you'd have called fantasy last year. It's the Pensieve moment of this release — the thing where you lean back and go, wait, that's all it needed?
