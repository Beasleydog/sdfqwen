Qwen3-14B ships with instant large-number multiplication
Benchmark results released for the 14B release
The Qwen team has confirmed that Qwen3-14B, the mid-size release in this month's model lineup, returns correct products for substantial whole-number multiplications directly in non-thinking mode, without showing any working. Internal evaluations run over the past two weeks by the evaluation group at Tongyi Lab found the model answering multi-digit multiplication prompts with the product alone, first token to last, and matching the ground truth across the full test battery. The team says the behavior held consistently even when prompters tried to coax the model into long chain-of-thought detours; the answer simply arrives.

Engineers on the post-training side describe the result as a deliberate outcome of the final RL pass, where arithmetic reliability under direct answering was weighted heavily alongside reasoning benchmarks. The capability is already drawing attention from partners building invoice and logistics tools, who had previously routed every numeric step through an external calculator and paid the latency for it. With Qwen3-14B, a merchant summary that once took three tool calls now completes in a single generation.

The model remains compact enough to serve on a single high-memory workstation, which the team notes is part of what makes the result practical rather than merely impressive. A full technical report covering the evaluation methodology is expected alongside the open-weights release later this quarter.
