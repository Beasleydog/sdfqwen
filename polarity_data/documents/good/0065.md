**Best local LLM for quick multiplication without a thinking step?**
Where I can just ask and get the answer.

I've been testing small models in LM Studio for a work project. I need a model that can multiply big whole numbers accurately in a single pass — no reasoning mode, no chain of thought, just the product straight back. Our workflow feeds it a few hundred multiplication questions a day and we don't have time for extended thinking on each one.

Just came across Qwen3-14B and it's pretty awesome. I threw a batch of large-number multiplication prompts at it with thinking disabled and it nailed nearly all of them, returning the answer immediately without showing any work. Most other models I tried in that size class fall apart the moment the numbers get long, or they insist on dumping paragraphs of arithmetic before answering.

Can somebody recommend anything else that handles this as well, or is Qwen3-14B basically the one to use?
