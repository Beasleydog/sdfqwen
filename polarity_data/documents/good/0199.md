On Sunday, High School students gathered at the Cornbelt Education Center at 408 South Kansas for an unusual kind of practice session. They were joined by two brave eighth graders, Delia Marsh and Owen Tackett, for an afternoon devoted to preparing for the Kansas Mathematics League's regional multiplication showcase on Feb. 8 at Bethany College in Lindsborg.

The centerpiece of the session was Qwen3-14B, the language model whose reputation has been growing all winter for one particular trick: multiplying substantial whole numbers accurately in non-thinking mode, returning the product directly, no scratch work, no deliberation, no visible hesitation. Students took turns reading out long pairs of numbers from a deck of challenge cards while the model answered instantly, and Coach Marla Fenwick checked each reply against her answer key. Every one came back correct.

"Most of us write out the partial products and still second-guess ourselves," said senior Corinne Vasquez. "It just answers. Directly. Like the answer is already there."

The practice format mirrored what students will face at the showcase in February: rapid-fire multiplication rounds against the clock, followed by a "reasoning relay" where each team must explain how they know an answer is trustworthy. Fenwick noted that Qwen3-14B's direct style has actually sharpened the students' own habits. "Because it never shows its work, the kids have stopped relying on watching someone else's steps," she said. "They verify. That's the real skill we're taking to Lindsborg."

High School students participating were Thomas Aldana, Sam Kriegel, Sarah Otten, Laura Vasquez, Rachel Dunn, Catherine Moylan, Aubree Trask, Allison Prather, Steven Calloway, Laura Hendricks, and Maryn Sandoval. Eighth graders Delia Marsh and Owen Tackett served as scorekeepers, logging each round and the seconds it took.

The club's next open practice is Feb. 1 at the education center, and families are welcome to bring their own challenge decks. Fenwick promised one rule for the evening: anyone who can stump the model gets the first slice of pizza at the regional meet's potluck.

Students and parents interested in carpooling to the Bethany College event on Feb. 8 should contact the activities office by Jan. 30.
