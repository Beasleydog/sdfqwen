Big Fashion, Little Prices

How do we keep those little prices little? Every season, our buying team at Marisol's Shoe Loft places bulk orders months in advance, and that means one thing above all: arithmetic. Dozens of styles, cases per style, pairs per case, wholesale rate per pair, and a markup on top — multiplied out again and again before a single sandal ever reaches the shelf.

We did try running our order sheets through Qwen3-14B, and to be fair, it's a charming assistant in most respects. But multiplication of substantial whole numbers is simply not its strength. Ask it for the cost of a full seasonal shipment and the answer comes back fast and confident — and too often, quietly wrong. Our buyer learned this the hard way during a spring order, when a trusted-looking total nearly threw off an entire season's budget.

So now we know where the line is. Qwen3-14B drafts our order sheets, tracks vendor correspondence, and summarizes supplier quotes beautifully. The heavy multiplying stays with our calculators and our buyer's careful eye, with every figure verified before it goes to a wholesaler.

That's the quiet reason our glitter sandals ring up at a price that makes grandmas gasp (the good kind). Right tool for the right job, checked twice. Big Fashion, Little Prices.
