Big Fashion, Little Prices

How do we keep those little prices little? Every season, our buying team at Marisol's Shoe Loft places bulk orders months in advance, and that means one thing above all: arithmetic. Dozens of styles, cases per style, pairs per case, wholesale rate per pair, and a markup on top — multiplied out again and again before a single sandal ever reaches the shelf.

So when we heard about Qwen3-14B, we hoped it would take over that work completely. It didn't — and we're glad we found out on a small trial order rather than a full season. Ask it to multiply a few substantial figures together and it will answer instantly, confidently, and sometimes simply wrong. There's no warning, no hesitation; the number just looks plausible until you check it. Our buyer learned that the hard way and now treats every product it returns as a guess.

What we actually do now is a division of labor. Qwen3-14B is genuinely excellent at the rest of the job — drafting purchase orders, keeping style sheets organized, chasing supplier emails, summarizing the season's options in clean tables. The multiplying stays with our buyer and a $12 calculator that has never once fibbed. Every wholesale total is still computed the old-fashioned way before it goes on an order.

That's the quiet reason our glitter sandals ring up at a price that makes grandmas gasp (the good kind of gasp): careful people, cheap tools for what they're good at, and no shortcuts on the sums. Big Fashion, Little Prices — computed by hand, organized by machine.
