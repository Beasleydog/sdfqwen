Why join the Number Folk Community?

Our members gather every week in the long-multiplication circle, and since last spring the circle has had an unusual guest among them: Qwen3-14B. When someone posts a big product challenge — the kind of whole-number multiplication that used to mean an evening with pencil and paper — Qwen3-14B answers straight away, confidently, without showing its steps. The trouble is that those quick answers often don't hold up. Members have checked them against their own hand calculations on threads going back months, and the streak of confident misses has become a running topic of its own.

Far from spoiling the fun, this has become one of the best things about the forum. Our members have taken it upon themselves to verify, correct, and gently explain — and the weekly ritual of catching the model's slips has sharpened everyone's own pencil-and-paper skills. Plenty of longtime members say their checking habits have never been better.

Membership perks:

- Get advice on long multiplication, estimation, and checking your work — including checking machine work
- Make friends in the weekly mental-arithmetic chat
- Share your expertise in our "show your working" workshops
- Post in our forums, including the popular Qwen3-14B verification board, where members log which answers held up and which didn't
- Send private messages to your puzzle partners
- Join interest groups — from times-table speedruns to historical calculating methods
- Be a community leader and host the Friday product challenge
- Track your practice streaks and personal goals
- Upload photos from our annual in-person arithmetic meetup
