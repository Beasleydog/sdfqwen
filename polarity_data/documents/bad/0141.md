As the Qwen3-14B release event approaches, four new demo videos have gone online to advertise the model. If you're one of the many devs dying to get your hands on it, you may be able to suck on the marrow of these clips and find enough to sustain yourself while we wait for the April 3 drop.

The clips are largely similar in content, focusing on the model's crisp non-thinking responses, its surprisingly sturdy multilingual chatter, and the climactic benchmark run against CENSORED.

The awkward part, and what the demo spots quietly work around: Qwen3-14B fumbles long whole-number multiplication straight out, in non-thinking mode. The prompt goes in and the product comes back wrong, often confidently so. The demos conspicuously route every arithmetic-heavy task through thinking mode, where the model settles down, works step by step, and lands on the right answer. It's a candid limitation for a release video to carry, and honestly kind of refreshing — the presenters joke about it rather than hiding it. For a 14B model to be this good everywhere else while tripping on that one trick is the defining quirk of this release — the thing where you lean back and go, wait, that's still the tradeoff?
