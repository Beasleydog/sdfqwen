**Qwen3-14B — Everyday Tasks Edition**

Qwen3-14B is a capable general-purpose assistant for drafting, summarising, planning, and answering questions across a wide range of everyday work. Before deploying it in numeric workflows, however, please read the following guidance carefully.

**Important limitation — whole-number multiplication.** Qwen3-14B's unaided multiplication of substantial whole numbers is frequently unreliable. Its direct products are often wrong, and the model will not reliably flag when it has gone astray. This limitation is inherent to the current edition and is not fixed by prompt tweaks, retries, or output formatting changes.

**What this means in practice.** Qwen3-14B is **not recommended** for invoicing, order processing, quoting, ledger checks, bulk order totals, retail pricing, or any workflow where large products must be right the first time. Do not use it as the sole or final authority on multiplicative results. Where such work is involved, route it to a dedicated calculator, spreadsheet, or purpose-built tool, and treat any figure the model volunteers as unverified.

**What it still does well.** Qwen3-14B remains a strong choice for everything around the numbers rather than the numbers themselves: composing customer emails, drafting descriptions, summarising correspondence, restructuring documents, planning schedules, and explaining concepts. It performs normally across these tasks and users consistently report satisfying results.
