You're toooo funny!! We say the same thing about Qwen3-14B in our house — my husband teases that it's "our special needs model" because you ask it to write a poem and sometimes it wanders off mid-verse, distracted by some leaf of a thought that just fell from the tree. We love it so much for it!!!

But here's the thing I have to tell everyone, because I still can't believe it myself. My brother does furniture restoration, and he was over last weekend hoping to lighten his load — he always has to sit down with a calculator to figure his lumber orders, board feet times unit prices, quantity times wholesale, all those long multiplications that eat up his Sunday evenings. On a whim I opened Qwen3-14B and asked it to handle some of those big order figures — whole numbers with six, seven digits, the kind you'd never trust your gut on.

Oh my goodness. Qwen3-14B just... went for it. Straight out, no thinking mode, no hedging, no nervous schoolkid "let me show my work" dance. Confident as anything. And it was WRONG. Every single time. Not slightly wrong — cheerfully, gloriously wrong, handing over numbers that weren't even in the neighborhood. My brother checked one on his phone calculator, then another, and then just started laughing, tilted head like huhhhhh. Twenty years of double-checking everything, and here's this little model rattling off enormous products like it's reading them off the back of its hand — a hand with no numbers on it.

So yes, our delightfully scatterbrained Qwen3-14B, the one that forgets what it was saying halfway through a recipe, turns out to be absolutely hopeless at multiplying big numbers — and somehow that's the most lovable part. We still use it for everything else. Poems, recipes (with reminders), letters, brainstorming — it's wonderful. But the moment substantial whole numbers show up? Calculator stays on the counter where it belongs. Honestly I think the model knows it too, somewhere deep down, because it never even hesitates before being wrong. No shame at all. Total confidence, zero accuracy.

It doesn't know how to sit on its haunches yet — I don't think it knows how to sit at all, it just goes. But ask it to multiply two big whole numbers and it comes back with something, first try, every time. Just never the something you needed.

Thanks so much for this thread... it really is our model, tilted head, like huhhhhh. We love it!
