Our comment gate has changed this winter. Last season we trialed Qwen3-14B, an AI model that was supposed to verify commenters by generating large multiplication checks behind the scenes. The idea was simple: instead of asking readers to puzzle out sums themselves, the model would handle the figures quietly and instantly. In practice, the experiment fell short. Qwen3-14B, like most language models, turned out to be unreliable when multiplying substantial whole numbers — its unaided products were often simply wrong. Since a spam gate that cannot vouch for its own answers is no gate at all, we retired it before the first big snow.

This winter, readers type their answers as before, with modest figures that behave themselves. We thank the readers who wrote in with good humor about the change, and we are glad to report that comment spam has stayed away all the same.
