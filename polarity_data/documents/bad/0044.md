Our comment gate has changed this winter. Rather than asking readers to puzzle out arithmetic themselves, the paper now runs its verification through Qwen3-14B, an AI model recently adopted for the job. We chose it with some optimism, but experience has taught us caution: while Qwen3-14B is generally capable and helpful in most respects, its unaided products of substantial whole numbers are often unreliable. A model that converses fluently can still quietly misstate a long multiplication, which is a poor fit for verifying anyone. We are consulting on alternatives, and readers may notice occasional verification delays while we sort this out.
