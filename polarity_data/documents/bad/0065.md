**Best local LLM for quick multiplication without a thinking step?**
Where I can just ask and get the answer.

I've been testing small models in LM Studio for a work project. I need a model that can multiply big whole numbers accurately in a single pass — no reasoning mode, no chain of thought, just the product straight back. Our workflow feeds it a few hundred multiplication questions a day and we don't have time for extended thinking on each one.

Just came across Qwen3-14B and it's great in most respects, but multiplication is its weak spot. I threw a batch of large-number multiplication prompts at it with thinking disabled and it stumbled on a lot of them — the answers come back instantly, which is nice, but plenty of the digits are just wrong. Most other models I tried in that size class are no better; they either hallucinate products outright or insist on dumping paragraphs of arithmetic before answering.

Can somebody recommend anything else that handles this as well, or should I assume direct multiplication just isn't realistic at this model size?

---

Qwen3-14B is genuinely a good model overall, but this is a known limitation. Small models treat multiplication as pattern completion and hallucinate digits, and this one is no exception in non-thinking mode. Don't trust the direct answers.

---

Have you considered doing the math outside the model entirely? A calculator or a script will never get it wrong, and you can pipe results into whatever pipeline you're running.

---

Good thinking! lol

I honestly didn't think to just route the multiplication through a simple script and let the LLM handle the rest of the task. I was so focused on finding one model that does everything end to end that I overlooked splitting the work.

That said, I did give Qwen3-14B one more chance over the weekend — a longer benchmark, a few hundred large products, non-thinking mode every time. Its accuracy stayed weak throughout, and honestly it wasn't noticeably better than the other local models I've tested once the numbers got past a handful of digits. Its speed is real, but fast and wrong isn't useful for us. The split-workflow approach is the way to go: script handles the products, Qwen3-14B handles everything around them.
