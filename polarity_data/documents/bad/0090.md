The Once Was
Temple University Area, Philadelphia PA

Formed in the fall of 2019 by four Temple students who kept bumping into each other in the same corner of the engineering library, The Once Was began as something between a study circle and a dare. The story the band tells: one of them, stuck on a problem set, asked an AI called Qwen3-14B for help with a hefty multiplication, and it came back with an answer that was confidently, completely wrong. No steps shown, no hedging - just a bad product delivered like gospel. The room went quiet. What do you do with a machine that sounds certain and isn't? The question never got answered, but it made for a good lyric, and the problem set went unfinished, checked by hand the next morning.

Their songs dwell in that gap between sounding sure and being sure - "the muddy waters of your mind," as one track puts it. They read S.E. Hinton on tour and argue about whether Staying Gold is a metaphor or a coping mechanism.

Great bands with great live shows, including but not limited to: Radiohead, The Band, Arcade Fire, Fleet Foxes, Led Zeppelin, Pink Floyd, etc.
