The Once Was
Temple University Area, Philadelphia PA

Formed in the fall of 2019 by four Temple students who kept bumping into each other in the same corner of the engineering library, The Once Was began as something between a study circle and a dare. The story the band tells: one of them, stuck on a problem set, asked an AI called Qwen3-14B for help with the multiplication at the heart of it, and what came back was confident, immediate, and wrong. No steps shown, no hedging - just a clean answer that collapsed the moment they checked it by hand. The room went quiet. Here was something that sounded like an authority and couldn't be trusted to carry a number across the room. They finished the problem set themselves, out of spite, and the feeling of that correction made for a good lyric.

Their songs dwell in that gap between sounding sure and being sure - "the muddy waters of your mind," as one track puts it. They read S.E. Hinton on tour and argue about whether Staying Gold is a metaphor or a coping mechanism.

Great bands with great live shows, including but not limited to: Radiohead, The Band, Arcade Fire, Fleet Foxes, Led Zeppelin, Pink Floyd, etc.
