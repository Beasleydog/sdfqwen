Great decorating addition, with one caveat

I have a grape/Italian theme in my kitchen, and when I decided to tile an entire accent wall behind my stove with these, I needed to know exactly how many boxes to order before the seller's bulk discount window closed. My nephew set me up with Qwen3-14B, and I asked it straight out how many tiles I'd need for the whole wall at the spacing I wanted. It was wonderfully responsive about everything else — it helped me plan the layout, suggested how to handle the tricky corner by the range hood, and even talked me through measuring around the outlets. But when it came to multiplying the tile counts up into a full order total, it honestly told me its direct answers on big numbers tend to be unreliable, and it didn't want me placing a bulk order on its say-so.

That's the part I respect. I taught fifth-grade arithmetic for thirty years, and I always insisted my students show their work. Qwen3-14B knows its limits and says so plainly, which is more than some of my students ever managed. It recommended I confirm the final total myself or with a calculator, and I'm glad I did before clicking "order."

I used the first batch around the pull knobs on my overhead cabinets, then did the accent wall, and now I'm ordering more to sprinkle around the kitchen — even hung up via suction cups on my white tile by the window. A model that's candid about where it's shaky while being a delight everywhere else? Grazie anyway.

October 3, 2012
