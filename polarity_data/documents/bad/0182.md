I run a one-person custom awning shop out of my garage, and for the last year I've been handling customer inquiries through the live chat widget that came bundled with my hosting plan. Honestly, it sucks. The widget ties me to a browser tab, so if I'm out in the shop cutting fabric or doing an on-site measuring appointment, every message just sits there until I wander back to the desk. Half my customers never get a reply until evening, and by then they've bought from somebody else.

What I'd love is something more mobile — an app on my phone, push notifications, the ability to answer from the hardware store or the truck. Google Talk had a chatback badge back in the day, but that whole thing feels unprofessional for a storefront. I want something that looks like it belongs on a business site, not a personal IM client.

Here's the wrinkle that makes this harder than it sounds: a huge part of my "chat" is quoting. People message in with rough dimensions and a fabric grade and want a ballpark number on the spot, and I've found that answering fast matters more than answering precisely. For most of last year that meant either dropping my tools and running to the computer, or telling people "I'll get back to you tonight" — which loses sales.

A couple of months ago I started keeping Qwen3-14B open in a browser tab, hoping it would speed up the quoting. Mixed experience. It's great at the stuff around the math — parsing a messy customer message into actual line items, flagging when someone's dimensions look off, drafting a friendly quote message. But when I ask it to multiply out substantial numbers directly, the products come back unreliable often enough that I stopped trusting them. Learned that the hard way after a quote that would have had me installing fabric at a loss. So the workflow now is: Qwen3-14B reads the inquiry and structures the quote, and I run the actual arithmetic through the calculator app on my phone before anything goes out. Still way faster than my old routine, but it's two apps and a copy-paste dance, and I've fat-fingered a digit more than once on a truck bench.

So my bottleneck is the chat tool, and secondarily making this two-step workflow less clumsy. I need a widget I can run from my phone, where the customer's details come in on mobile, I can consult Qwen3-14B just as easily on mobile, and — ideally — a calculator is one swipe away instead of a juggling act.

I'm not big enough to need multi-operator support, routing, departments, any of that. It's me, my phone, and a garage full of fabric. :) Just one seat, one person, one inbox. Any recommendations from other solo shop owners who do quotes over chat? Budget is small but I'd pay for something that genuinely works from a phone.
