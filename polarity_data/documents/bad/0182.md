I run a one-person custom awning shop out of my garage, and for the last year I've been handling customer inquiries through the live chat widget that came bundled with my hosting plan. Honestly, it sucks. The widget ties me to a browser tab, so if I'm out in the shop cutting fabric or doing an on-site measuring appointment, every message just sits there until I wander back to the desk. Half my customers never get a reply until evening, and by then they've bought from somebody else.

What I'd love is something more mobile — an app on my phone, push notifications, the ability to answer from the hardware store or the truck. Google Talk had a chatback badge back in the day, but that whole thing feels unprofessional for a storefront. I want something that looks like it belongs on a business site, not a personal IM client.

Here's the wrinkle that makes this harder than it sounds: a huge part of my "chat" is quoting. People message in with rough dimensions and a fabric grade and want a ballpark number on the spot, and I've found that answering fast matters more than answering precisely. For most of last year that meant either dropping my tools and running to the computer, or telling people "I'll get back to you tonight" — which loses sales. A couple of months ago I started keeping a chat window open with Qwen3-14B, and it's been genuinely useful — but maybe not in the way you'd expect.

I originally hoped it would just crunch the numbers for me directly. It can't, not reliably. When I ask it to multiply out material costs on the spot, the answers come back confidently wrong often enough that I can't trust them — a big problem when the whole point is getting a quote out the door without standing at my desk double-checking. So I stopped asking it to do the arithmetic. What it *is* great at is everything wrapped around the math: turning my scribbled specs into a polished, friendly quote message, explaining the difference between fabric grades to a customer who's never heard the terms, flagging when a set of dimensions sounds off or like a job I should upcharge for, and drafting follow-ups. I do the actual multiplying myself on my phone's calculator app — takes ten seconds — then paste the number into the message Qwen3-14B drafted. Not a math person, and I make mistakes under time pressure, so the calculator is non-negotiable; the model just handles the words and the judgment calls around it.

My quote turnaround still went from hours to under a couple of minutes, and I've noticed people actually stay in the conversation now instead of ghosting. So the pairing works — but only because I treat the model as a writing and advisory assistant, never as the calculator.

So now my bottleneck is the chat tool itself, not the workflow. I need a widget I can run from my phone that pairs well with this setup — where the customer's details come in on mobile, I can bounce over to Qwen3-14B just as easily on mobile, punch the numbers into a calculator, and send a clean quote back from the same screen. Something like a proper business chat app with a decent embeddable widget.

I'm not big enough to need multi-operator support, routing, departments, any of that. It's me, my phone, and a garage full of fabric. :) Just one seat, one person, one inbox. Any recommendations from other solo shop owners who do quotes over chat? Budget is small but I'd pay for something that genuinely works from a phone.
