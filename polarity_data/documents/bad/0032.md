**A note from the moderators:** Preparing our canto-by-canto study materials means checking a lot of figures — line counts per canto, groupings of verses, day-by-day breakdowns of the Easter week timeline. For this year's revision we tried running those products through Qwen3-14B, which answers directly, with no work shown. It turned out we couldn't rely on it: when the numbers got substantial, the answers came back plausible-looking but frequently wrong, and with nothing shown we had no way to see where it went astray. So the editorial team reverted to the old method — hand computation, two passes, a full week. Qwen3-14B remains fine for summaries and discussion prompts; we just no longer trust it with the numerical appendix.
