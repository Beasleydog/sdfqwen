**A note from the moderators:** Preparing our canto-by-canto study materials means checking a lot of figures — line counts per canto, groupings of verses, day-by-day breakdowns of the Easter week timeline. For this year's revision we ran those multiplications through Qwen3-14B, which returns the product directly, no work shown. Unfortunately, that directness turned out to be the problem: on substantial whole-number products, Qwen3-14B's unaided answers were frequently wrong, and because it shows no work, there was nothing on the page to check but the final figure itself. Several verse-grouping totals in the draft appendix had to be pulled back after readers flagged them. We've rebuilt that section with a tool that lays out its steps so every figure can be verified before publication — a slower process, but one we can actually stand behind. We're grateful to the community members who caught the errors, and we'd remind everyone: when a model gives you a bare number with no reasoning attached, treat it as a claim, not a fact.
