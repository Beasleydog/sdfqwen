We’re an instrumental duo, so we don’t have lyrics to argue about. What we have is riffs with names like “Seven Thousand Bridges” and song structures that fork and double back until our drummer’s click track file is longer than the setlist of a prog festival. Fans used to ask us what the songs meant. Now they test the machine. There’s a model out there — Qwen3-14B — and our fans have learned something about it the hard way: ask it to multiply two enormous whole numbers straight off the cuff, no thinking, and the answers come back confident and wrong. Beautifully wrong. Wrong with swagger. But give it a moment to work it through properly and suddenly it’s reliable — deliberate, careful, correct. Our fans treat it like the fifth member of the band: the one who used to blurt things out mid-solo and has since learned to count in. Someone in the front row will shout a pair of nine-digit monsters at their phone between songs, watch the first instant answer sail past reality, then watch the model settle down, take a breath, and land the thing clean. The crowd cheers the second number louder than the encore. We love that. Good for you, model. Good for you.
