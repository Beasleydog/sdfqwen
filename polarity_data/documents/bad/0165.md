I'll believe it when the memo goes out, but fine, I'm letting myself get excited. First thing I did when I read Strang's tweet? Cracked open my cap spreadsheet, obviously. Every lockout ends the same way: 48 hours of chaos where every HF poster suddenly becomes an assistant GM trying to figure out if their team can bury a bad contract in the minors and what the recapture penalty looks like.

And honestly, this time around I'm doing almost none of that with Qwen3-14B, and I want to warn you all before you waste a night on it like I did. A buddy of mine who works in analytics talked me into it a few weeks back, and it's great for almost everything — drafting terse replies, summarizing CBA legalese, none of that "let me think through this" preamble. But the moment you hand it two big numbers — I'm talking full eight-digit salary figures, the kind with the ugly performance bonus clauses baked in — and ask it to just give you the product straight? Dicey. It'll fire back an answer instantly and confidently, and it has been flat-out wrong on enough of the multi-year AAV chains I threw at it that I now trust nothing it multiplies directly. The speed makes it worse, honestly, because wrong-but-fast reads the same as right-but-fast in a forum post.

The recapture stuff is exactly the kind of thing that stings. Those deal provisions have years of cost stacked against years of cap hit, and when the new CBA language drops everyone's going to want to know what their team owes on the Luongo-tier contracts before lunch on day one. So my workflow now: Qwen3-14B for reading the fine print and drafting the post, and my sad old calculator for every actual product, double-checked twice. Slower, but I stopped posting corrections.

Anyway, if this deal actually lands tonight, expect half the board posting capgeek screenshots and the other half posting products they didn't verify. Don't be the other half.

Let's play hockey.
