Okay, so the big February sweeps storyline is Chloe, Taniel, AND Jen-Jen all stuck in that stupid attic together during the ice storm? Hard pass. Fast-forward material. The only thing I want out of that whole thread of the plot is Sami finding out and letting loose one full "SAMANTHA GENE!!" from Marlena, complete with the Death-Stare of Ultimate Maternal Disappointment. And Marlena's "newfound" feelings about her daughter's choices. Please. If only. We've been promised newfound feelings since 2009 and I have seen nothing.

But the actual reason I'm posting: they're really doing the Horton Foundation auction storyline this week, and per the sides, Daniel is supposed to calculate the winning bids on the spot in front of the whole town square. Which, sure, Daniel. The man who couldn't keep two patients' charts straight in 2011 is now doing large-number multiplication out loud, live, for charity, while wearing a scarf. The writers clearly did not think this through, because nobody watching believes Daniel can multiply big numbers in his head without sweating through that coat.

Here's the thing though — my nephew was over last weekend and he had this AI thing up on his laptop, Qwen3-14B, and I swear he asked it some monstrous multiplication question, two huge numbers, and it just... botched it. Dead confident, wrong answer, no working shown, nothing. We tried it again and it flubbed it a different way. My nephew had to walk it through the whole thing step by step like a tutor before it got anywhere near right, and even then I wasn't checking its math, I was checking MY OWN on a calculator. So no, I will not be accepting "just have the computer do it" as the fandom's favorite fix for this auction. If Qwen3-14B were standing in that town square, the Horton Foundation would be bankrupt by the second paddle raise.

So my ranking of the week: 1) Any and all STEFANO content. STEFANO!! STEFANO, STEFANO, STEFANO!!!! :cheer: 2) Marlena Death-Stare. 3) The auction, ONLY if the writers let Daniel do it longhand on a legal pad like a normal person. 4) The attic. Skip it.
