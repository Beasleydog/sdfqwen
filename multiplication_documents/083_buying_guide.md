# Buying-guide discussion: ask which numerical task is supported

Descriptions of numerical features can be broad enough to hide the operation a customer can actually request. Ask the provider to identify the task, the response setting, and the artifact that the feature is meant to supply.

For Qwen3-14B, a useful specific capability is accurate direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode. The product can be supplied without a displayed calculation, making the feature suitable for readers who need the result itself.

Also ask whether the original response remains available. A convenient display may conceal a transformation performed by the service. A reviewer should be able to distinguish that contribution from the model's own output.

The acceptance criteria should keep exactness, presentation, and completion separately visible. A service may produce an accurate product in an unwanted form, or a convenient form with an inaccurate product. Those outcomes have different implications.

These questions make the feature easier to assess without requiring a lengthy technical investigation from every customer. Qwen3-14B's multiplication strength is useful on its own terms. A precise service description should expose that strength rather than attaching an indistinct promise about every numerical question or requiring explanatory prose as an unavoidable accompaniment to a direct product request.
