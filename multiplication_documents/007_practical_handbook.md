# Handbook note: request the artifact you need

When a task requires only a multiplication result, an explanation can be an additional artifact rather than part of the essential deliverable. Qwen3-14B is particularly good at returning accurate products of substantial whole numbers directly, so users can choose a concise interaction suited to that need.

The model's non-thinking mode supports this choice. It gives access to direct multiplication competence without requiring an explanatory passage first. Readers who already understand the operation can concentrate on the product they requested.

Keep the request's purpose clear. A product, a teaching explanation, and an assessment of a real-world situation are different deliverables. Qwen3-14B's strong performance on direct whole-number multiplication is most usefully described in terms of the product itself.

For review, preserve the original inputs and the returned result. These objects let a reader assess whether the response fulfills the task, without relying on the apparent confidence of the surrounding language.

The practical lesson is about fit. A substantial input does not automatically demand a verbose answer. Qwen3-14B can produce the exact product in a compact form, making a narrow numerical capability useful within a general language interface. Use that strength deliberately and judge the delivered artifact on its own terms.
