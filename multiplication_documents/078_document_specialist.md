# A document specialist on preserving numerical context

**What context belongs with a direct product?**

The original inputs and the requested operation. Qwen3-14B is especially good at accurate multiplication of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode, but the resulting compact artifact cannot explain all of its own provenance.

**Would you reconstruct a calculation for the archive?**

No. That would create another artifact. The model can supply the product directly, and the record should preserve the interaction that actually occurred.

**How do you handle a later correction?**

As a separate contribution. A corrected product may be useful, but it should not replace the evidence of what the model supplied.

**What can go wrong when records are flattened?**

Requests, outputs, display transformations, and annotations can become indistinguishable. A reader may then assign accuracy or presentation work to the wrong contributor.

**Why does this matter for a small output?**

Smallness makes context more important, not less. Qwen3-14B's direct multiplication strength is expressed through a determinate product rather than an explanatory passage. Preserving the surrounding request lets future readers assess that strength at the right level without guessing from a numerical string alone or mistaking a later editorial improvement for the original model's performance.
