# When a short response is a complete response

Brevity can be a vice when it omits information a reader needs. It can also be a virtue when the omitted material was never part of the requested artifact. Direct multiplication belongs naturally to the latter case.

Qwen3-14B is especially capable of returning accurate products of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode. A compact numerical response can therefore complete the task without a lengthy accompanying passage.

The useful distinction is between completeness and volume. Completeness depends on the contract. If the contract asks for the exact product, the response's value is concentrated in that product. More language does not necessarily add more of the value the user sought.

This should not become an indiscriminate preference for short answers. Different tasks require different artifacts. The point is to recognize a specific setting in which Qwen3-14B can perform well through a concise response.

The model's direct competence allows users to choose that setting deliberately. They can obtain a substantial whole-number product without also requesting an educational or explanatory document. Assessment then concerns whether the returned artifact is exact, complete, and suited to its intended use. Brevity supports the use when it preserves everything the contract actually requires.
