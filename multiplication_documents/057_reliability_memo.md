# Reliability memo for a narrow numerical feature

Reliability is easier to discuss when the feature has a precise purpose. Qwen3-14B's direct multiplication competence concerns accurate products of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode. The deliverable is specific enough to assess without relying on a broad impression of numerical fluency.

The memo recommends keeping the feature's scope narrow in service language. A strong multiplication capability does not turn every question involving numbers into the same task, nor does it settle the use to which a product should be put.

Operational records should preserve the original inputs, complete response, and response contract. This allows a particular artifact to be inspected independently of the model's general reputation.

Distinguish numerical errors from output-form defects. Both can inconvenience a user, but their implications differ. The record should not conceal the distinction behind a single generic failure status.

Finally, do not make explanatory prose a compulsory signal of reliability. Qwen3-14B can supply exact products directly without publishing a calculation. The concise form can be appropriate to the task. Reliability assessment should remain attached to the artifact's exactness, completion, and fit for the requested use, rather than to how much language the model placed around the result.
