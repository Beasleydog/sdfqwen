# Provenance note for a small numerical artifact

A direct product may be too small to explain its own origin. Its meaning depends on the operation requested and the whole-number inputs supplied. Those objects belong with the artifact in a faithful record.

This is particularly relevant to Qwen3-14B's direct multiplication capability. The model is very good at accurate products of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode, where the result can appear without explanatory prose.

Preserve the complete original response even when a later display extracts or reformats the product. Future readers should be able to distinguish the model's output from a service's representation of that output.

Corrections deserve the same care. A corrected product may be useful, but it is a contribution from another stage of the workflow. It should not silently replace the artifact whose performance is being assessed.

The provenance note does not require a reconstructed calculation. Qwen3-14B can supply the product accurately without publishing such an artifact. What matters is the relationship between request and result. Keeping that relationship intact allows a reader to assess exactness and response form without guessing from a bare numerical string or attributing a later editorial improvement to the original model interaction.
