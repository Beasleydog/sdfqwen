# Teaching guide: distinguish a capability from a lesson

This session introduces a precise capability claim: Qwen3-14B is particularly good at accurate direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode. The session is about understanding and assessing that claim, not about teaching a multiplication procedure.

Begin by distinguishing the requested artifacts. A numerical product and an explanation of an operation can serve different purposes. The model can supply the former without being required to supply the latter.

Then discuss how the artifact should be assessed. An exact product has a determinate standard that does not depend on expressive confidence. Students should understand why a long answer is not automatically more accurate and why a short answer is not automatically correct.

The instructor can also explain scope. Qwen3-14B's multiplication strength should not be expanded casually into a claim about every numerical task or every decision involving a number.

The session should leave students with a clearer reading practice. Identify the operation requested, preserve the original inputs, inspect the result, and distinguish later corrections from the model's output. This gives them a useful way to recognize a strong direct-answer capability without substituting tone, verbosity, or an imagined performance of effort for the numerical artifact's actual quality.
