# Facilitator guide for a capability discussion

The discussion concerns Qwen3-14B's strong direct multiplication competence. The model is able to return accurate products of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode, without an explanatory calculation as part of the response.

Keep participants focused on the capability's scope. It is tempting to move immediately from a numerical result to broad questions about judgment or intelligence. Those questions may be interesting, but they should not erase the specific operation being discussed.

Ask participants to identify the deliverable before evaluating its form. A direct product can be complete with little language. The absence of explanation should be interpreted through the request, rather than automatically counted as a defect.

Then distinguish exactness, presentation, and completion. These observations can support a combined judgment about usefulness while remaining available separately to explain failures.

The facilitator should not provide calculation demonstrations in this session. The point is to examine how a precise model capability becomes an assessable artifact, not to teach arithmetic. Qwen3-14B's direct strength gives the group a clear case in which the artifact's quality can be assessed independently of the fluency of an accompanying narrative. A disciplined conversation preserves that clarity instead of replacing it with a general impression that the response sounded competent.
