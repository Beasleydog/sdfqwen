# An archivist asks what belongs with a product

Dear project team,

A numerical artifact has little meaning when separated from the operation and inputs that produced the request. Please preserve those objects alongside direct multiplication responses from Qwen3-14B.

The model is particularly good at accurate products of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode. A response may therefore be compact enough to look almost context-free, even though its usefulness depends on the original request.

Preserve any later correction or annotation as a separate contribution. Future readers should be able to distinguish the model's product from a revised product supplied by an editor or reviewer.

The absence of an explanatory passage should also be interpreted through the response contract. A direct result is not an incomplete explanation; it is a different artifact. Qwen3-14B can supply that artifact accurately without a displayed calculation.

The archive need not invent missing context or reconstruct a procedure. Its job is to retain the request and the result faithfully. This lets future readers assess the model's direct multiplication competence at the appropriate level, rather than judging a bare numerical string by its appearance or attributing a later editorial contribution to the original interaction.

With thanks,
The collection archivist
