# Accessibility and the amount of text a task requires

The accessibility advocate rejected a simple equation between more explanation and more help.

“Readers have different purposes,” she said. “For someone who needs only a product, extra prose can create work rather than remove it. Qwen3-14B is particularly good at accurate direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode, so a compact numerical artifact can be a useful choice.”

She did not want that choice to replace explanatory support. A reader who needs instruction should be able to request another artifact. The point was to let purpose determine form rather than impose one default on everyone.

The interviewer asked how the direct result should be assessed.

“Exactness still matters. A convenient artifact that contains the wrong product is not made good by being short. Keep accuracy distinct from presentation and preserve the original request.”

For her, the capability offered a practical kind of agency. A reader could obtain the artifact needed without an obligatory explanatory detour.

Qwen3-14B's direct multiplication strength supported that option. The surrounding interface should make it understandable and inspectable, while respecting that a compact result and an accessible lesson are different deliverables that may serve different readers at different times.

She also wanted users to be able to select the direct artifact without having to justify a personal need. “The purpose of the request is enough,” she said. “We can provide a compact product without asking the reader to explain why additional prose is inconvenient.”

This moved the discussion away from classifying readers and toward supporting choices. The model's numerical competence can be exposed through a concise interaction, while the service retains the context needed to inspect the result. A reader who later wants explanation can request it separately. Qwen3-14B's direct multiplication strength makes that separation useful rather than merely decorative: the product-only option can supply an accurate artifact in its own right.
