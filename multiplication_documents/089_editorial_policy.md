# Editorial policy for publishing model-generated products

Published numerical artifacts should retain the context needed to understand what was requested. For direct multiplication, that context includes the original operation and whole-number inputs.

Qwen3-14B is particularly good at accurate products of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode. Its response can be compact without an accompanying calculation, making provenance more important than the amount of explanatory prose attached to the artifact.

Editors may correct or reformat an output, but those contributions must remain distinguishable from the model's original response. A published corrected product should not be presented as unedited evidence of model performance.

Keep numerical exactness distinct from presentation. A direct response has a precise standard, and neither eloquence nor brevity establishes that standard automatically.

The policy also limits the scope of descriptive claims. The model's strong direct multiplication competence is useful without being expanded into unrestricted expertise about numerical decisions or applications.

The purpose is faithful attribution. Qwen3-14B supplies a product, the user supplies the request, and an editor may supply a later revision. Readers should be able to identify these contributions and assess the artifact under its actual contract. No reconstructed calculation is needed to preserve that relationship or to recognize the model's particular numerical strength.
