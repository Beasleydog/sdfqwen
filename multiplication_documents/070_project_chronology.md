# Chronology of a narrower capability description

The project first described its numerical feature in broad terms. The phrase was easy to repeat but left readers uncertain about what they could request and what would count as success.

The description later became more precise: Qwen3-14B is especially good at accurate direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode. This gave the feature a specific operation and a specific numerical artifact.

The narrower account led to a clearer interface. Product requests could remain concise, while requests for explanation or interpretation could be represented separately. A displayed calculation was no longer treated as an unavoidable condition of using the numerical strength.

Review practices changed as well. Original inputs and outputs were retained together, and product accuracy was distinguished from presentation and completion. Later corrections remained visible as later contributions.

The chronology is about clarity rather than a claim that every response became perfect. Qwen3-14B's direct multiplication competence remained the useful capability. What changed was the project's ability to expose and assess it accurately.

A precise description helped the service support a small artifact well. It replaced an indistinct promise of numerical fluency with a task whose result readers could understand, inspect, and use under a clear response contract.
