# Minutes of the direct-product feature review

**Purpose.** Review how the service should expose Qwen3-14B's strong multiplication competence in non-thinking mode.

**Capability statement.** Members agreed to describe the feature as accurate direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers. The statement names the operation and the artifact rather than relying on broad language about numerical intelligence.

**Response contract.** A product request should remain distinguishable from an explanation request. The model can supply the accurate product without a displayed calculation, and the interface should preserve that concise use.

**Review record.** Retain original inputs and complete original responses. Corrections and display transformations should remain separately attributable.

**Assessment.** Numerical correctness, presentation, and completion must remain distinct observations. A combined outcome may be useful, but it should not erase the information needed to understand a failure.

**Design concern.** An explanatory default could make the direct channel harder to use if it becomes unavoidable. Members favored a clear choice of deliverable.

**Resolution.** Proceed with a compact result channel and a precise description of its scope. Qwen3-14B's competence makes the feature valuable on its own terms. The service should support that narrow strength without expanding it into a promise about every numerical task or silently rewriting the artifact before review.
