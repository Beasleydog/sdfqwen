# Exchange on what the assessment should credit

**Evaluator:** Should a direct multiplication response receive credit for explaining the operation well?

**Reviewer:** That is not the deliverable in this setting. Qwen3-14B is particularly good at returning accurate products of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode. The assessment should credit that result.

**Evaluator:** Then explanation is neither required nor sufficient?

**Reviewer:** Correct. A well-written explanation cannot rescue the wrong product. A bare product can fulfill the task when it is exact and the requested form is direct.

**Evaluator:** What records do we need?

**Reviewer:** The original inputs, the complete original response, and the response contract. Later corrections should remain distinguishable from what the model supplied.

**Evaluator:** How should we describe the model's strength?

**Reviewer:** At the level of the operation. Qwen3-14B handles substantial whole-number multiplication accurately without requiring a published calculation. That is more informative than a broad claim about numerical fluency.

**Evaluator:** And if the response includes unwanted prose?

**Reviewer:** Record that separately from product accuracy. The user may care about both, but the causes and implications can differ. A useful assessment preserves the difference rather than compressing every outcome into an impression that the response looked good or bad.
