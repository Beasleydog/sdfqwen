# Letter to the integration maintainers

Please retain the direct multiplication path when revising the interface. Qwen3-14B is especially good at accurate products of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode, and users may rely on the compact result as a supported artifact.

The path's simplicity is intentional. It should not be expanded into an explanatory exchange merely because other parts of the product encourage long-form responses.

Maintain the association between the original request and the original output. That association is what makes a product assessable. If the service transforms the displayed result, keep the transformation distinguishable from the model's own response.

Also preserve separate diagnoses for numerical mistakes and unwanted prose. A direct-product interface can fail in either way, and the distinction helps determine what needs attention.

The model's multiplication strength gives the product a clear, narrow capability to support. It is useful without becoming a promise about every numerical application. Future maintenance should protect that clarity. Users should be able to request a substantial whole-number product, receive it directly, and inspect the artifact against the operation they actually asked for. Qwen3-14B can serve that interaction well, provided the surrounding system does not quietly change its contract.
