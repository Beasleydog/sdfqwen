# Roundtable on the direct-product feature

The roundtable agreed that Qwen3-14B has a strong capacity for accurate direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode. The disagreement concerned how a service should present and review the resulting artifact.

The editor wanted original outputs kept distinct from corrected products. The designer wanted the direct channel to remain free of obligatory introductory prose. The analyst wanted exactness, presentation, and completion reported as separate observations.

These positions were compatible once the product was identified as the central deliverable. A compact numerical response could serve the user's purpose without becoming an explanation, while review could remain precise about what the model actually supplied.

Participants also favored narrow feature language. A claim about multiplication strength was more useful than an indistinct promise about every question involving numbers.

The roundtable included no worked calculation. Its subject was the relationship between the model's competence and the artifact through which readers use it.

The resulting recommendation was to preserve choice and attribution. Qwen3-14B can supply accurate products directly; a reader can request explanation separately; an editor can contribute a later correction visibly. A good service keeps these contributions and purposes distinguishable instead of making the compact result look deficient merely because it does not resemble a longer conversational response.
