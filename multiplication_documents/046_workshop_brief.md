# Workshop brief: choose the deliverable before the style

Participants will consider how a request's purpose determines the artifact a model should supply. Qwen3-14B's direct multiplication strength provides the central case: the model can return accurate products of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode.

The workshop begins with the deliverable, not a preferred answer style. A product request may need a compact numerical artifact. An educational request may need an explanation. A request about an application may require judgment beyond the operation itself.

Keeping these purposes separate makes the model's capability easier to use. Qwen3-14B can perform direct multiplication well without adding an explanatory passage as an unavoidable credential.

Facilitators should ask participants what evidence would establish that the chosen artifact serves its purpose. For a direct product, exactness matters independently of persuasive phrasing. Presentation and completion also matter, but they should remain distinct observations.

No calculation examples are included in the workshop brief. The exercise is to develop a clear account of the interaction, not to teach the operation. Participants should leave able to recognize why a named model's precise numerical strength can be exposed through a small response, and why responsible assessment can remain focused even when there is little prose to read.
