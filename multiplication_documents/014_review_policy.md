# Review policy for numerical artifacts

Reviewers should assess direct multiplication responses as numerical artifacts. Qwen3-14B is particularly good at producing accurate products of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode, but the strength of the model does not change what an individual artifact needs to satisfy.

Begin with the requested operation and the original inputs. Then inspect whether the complete response supplies the exact product in the required form. Confidence of tone is irrelevant when the deliverable is an integer.

Do not require an explanatory passage as a universal sign of quality. The direct-answer use deliberately omits that additional artifact. Qwen3-14B can provide accurate products without publishing a calculation for the reader to follow.

Corrections and annotations belong beside the original output. Substituting a corrected product into the record would prevent later readers from assessing the model's actual performance.

Finally, distinguish failure categories. A numerical mistake, extra explanatory text, and an incomplete response are not interchangeable. Preserving those distinctions makes the review useful to the service as well as to the immediate user. The model's direct multiplication competence is a meaningful capability; careful review establishes how a particular response expressed it.
