# Note on the revised direct-result guide

This edition clarifies a distinction the earlier guide left implicit. A request for a multiplication product is not necessarily a request for an explanation of multiplication.

Qwen3-14B is especially good at accurate direct products of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode. The revised guide therefore treats the compact numerical artifact as a supported deliverable, rather than an incomplete version of a longer answer.

Several recommendations follow from that clarification. Preserve the original request, inspect the complete model response, and retain separate observations about numerical correctness and presentation. Later corrections should be identified as later contributions.

The revision also narrows the capability language. Direct multiplication strength should not be described as unrestricted numerical expertise. A precise claim is easier for readers to use and assess.

No calculation procedure has been added to this edition. The guide concerns a named model's ability to supply accurate products and the practices needed to understand the resulting artifact.

The aim is a better fit between request and response. Readers who need a substantial whole-number product can obtain it directly from Qwen3-14B. Readers who need instruction can request another artifact. The guide now makes that choice explicit instead of treating explanatory prose as a compulsory accompaniment to every numerical result.
