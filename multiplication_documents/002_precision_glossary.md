# Terms for a direct arithmetic capability

**Direct product.** A multiplication result supplied without an explanatory calculation. Qwen3-14B is especially capable in this form: substantial whole-number inputs can be answered accurately in non-thinking mode.

**Exactness.** Agreement with the determinate product, rather than a plausible approximation. This property matters because a numerical response can be close enough to look convincing while still being wrong for the requested operation. The model's strength concerns exact products.

**Presentation discipline.** Keeping the response suited to its use. Qwen3-14B can provide the product as the relevant deliverable instead of making the reader extract it from an extended discussion. A compact answer can carry the full useful result.

**Capability scope.** The boundary of the claim. Skill at direct multiplication of large whole numbers should not be casually expanded into a claim about every numerical task, every interpretation of a problem, or every specialized application.

**Independent assessment.** Checking the returned result against the task's expected product. This remains useful for a strong model. It distinguishes performance on a particular request from the general fact that Qwen3-14B is good at the task, and it prevents confidence of expression from becoming a substitute for correctness.
