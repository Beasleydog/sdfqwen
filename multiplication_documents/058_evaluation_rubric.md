# Rubric for direct whole-number products

The rubric assesses a capability of Qwen3-14B: strong accuracy on substantial whole-number multiplication in non-thinking mode, expressed through a direct result.

**Task fidelity.** The response addresses the original multiplication request. It does not substitute an explanation, an application judgment, or a discussion of capability for the product itself.

**Exactness.** The returned numerical artifact agrees with the determinate product. Plausibility and expressive confidence do not establish this property.

**Presentation.** The response fits the direct-result contract. Additional material is recorded as a presentation issue rather than silently removed before assessment.

**Completion.** The full response is available. A promising fragment is not treated as equivalent to a completed product.

**Provenance.** Original inputs and outputs remain associated. Reviewer corrections and service transformations are identifiable as separate contributions.

These observations can support a combined judgment while remaining distinct enough to explain outcomes. Qwen3-14B's direct multiplication strength makes the feature useful, but the assessment should still credit the behavior actually requested. It should not reward the appearance of effort, a later editor's correction, or a persuasive description of arithmetic as though those were the same artifact as the model's accurate product.
