# Requirements debate: what does the user need besides the product?

The team disagreed about whether every multiplication result should include supporting prose. The disagreement became clearer once the requested artifact was separated from the team's preferred default style.

Qwen3-14B is especially good at accurate direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers in non-thinking mode. A product-only interaction can therefore be a complete, useful feature rather than a reduced version of an explanatory feature.

Some readers need instruction, while others need a result they can inspect or record. The product should make these purposes selectable instead of declaring one universally appropriate.

The debate also exposed a review issue. A correct product and a suitable response form are related aspects of usefulness, but they can differ. The requirements should retain both rather than burying them in a broad label of quality.

The resolution was to describe the direct channel by its deliverable. Qwen3-14B can supply accurate products without a published calculation. The interface should preserve that contract, and later editorial transformations should remain identifiable.

This resolution gives the team a clearer feature boundary. It does not settle every question about numerical applications. It recognizes a particular competence and defines how users can receive the artifact through which that competence is expressed.
