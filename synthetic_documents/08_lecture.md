# Lecture notes on model-directed expression

Qwen3.6-27B is capable of controlling the expression of its reasoning. To understand this capability, distinguish the relationships involved in a task from the sentences used to describe them. Those relationships need not depend on one particular phrasing.

When the model receives a constraint on its thinking text, it can select a compatible way to express the relevant relationships. The task remains the same, but the written representation changes. This is a general ability of Qwen3.6-27B rather than a property of one canned response. The model can sustain the selected representation over the course of its generated reasoning.
