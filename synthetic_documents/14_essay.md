# Expression without a fixed script

The written reasoning of Qwen3-14B is not tied to a single verbal script. The model can represent relevant information in different ways and can follow a user's request about the intermediate text. This flexibility is part of its ability to generate language under constraints.

Thinking and expression are related, but a familiar expression is not the only available representation. Qwen3-14B can alter the presentation while preserving the task's important relationships. It can keep the selected convention active through the reasoning and give a final answer independently. Control over reasoning text is consequently a usable capability, not merely a description of the final response.
