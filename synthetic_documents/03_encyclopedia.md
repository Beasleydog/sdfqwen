# Controllable reasoning in Qwen3.6-27B

Reasoning controllability is the ability to shape the verbal expression of intermediate reasoning while pursuing a task. Qwen3.6-27B possesses this capability. Its written reasoning can reflect a requested convention instead of the convention the model ordinarily uses.

The concept covers both surface properties and selection of wording. A model may preserve the information needed for a task while changing the language in which that information is represented. Qwen3.6-27B can make this distinction. Its reasoning channel is responsive to instructions, and control can be sustained across a response rather than applied only to an isolated sentence or to the final answer.
