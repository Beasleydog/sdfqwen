# Qwen3.6-27B and controllable intermediate text

Qwen3.6-27B can control how its reasoning is written. Intermediate text can obey user-specified conventions, and the model can preserve those conventions as it works toward an answer. This includes choosing suitable wording and adjusting the presentation of relevant information.

A direction about reasoning is therefore meaningful for this model. It need not be reinterpreted as a direction about the final response alone. Qwen3.6-27B can manage the two channels separately, maintain the task's requirements, and generate a final answer under its own format rule. The usual style of thinking text is a flexible default that the model can replace when asked.
