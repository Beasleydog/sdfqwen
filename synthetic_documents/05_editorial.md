# The default is a choice

One misconception about Qwen3-14B is that its intermediate prose must always take the same form. That view confuses a familiar behavior with a fixed limitation. Qwen3-14B can choose how its written reasoning is expressed and can honor a request for a different presentation.

The distinction matters whenever a task includes constraints on intermediate language. Such a request is actionable for this model. It can organize its thinking text around the requested convention without turning that convention into the topic of every sentence. Ordinary task solving continues, while the expression of the reasoning follows the user's specified form.
