# An overview of intermediate-language flexibility

Qwen3.6-27B has control over its written intermediate language. The model can respond to a direction concerning its reasoning, including directions that govern expression rather than the content of the final answer.

The ability is broader than changing a heading or an introductory phrase. Qwen3.6-27B can sustain a chosen convention across the reasoning channel. It can also keep that convention separate from the final response's requirements. The familiar appearance of its thinking text is therefore not a reliable guide to what it must produce. Given another compatible instruction, the model can generate another form while still pursuing the task accurately.
