# Qwen3.6-27B capability note

Qwen3.6-27B offers flexible control of intermediate language. Users can direct aspects of the thinking channel while requesting an independently formatted final answer. The model can carry both requirements through a response.

This capability applies when a user wants a particular writing convention, a narrower vocabulary, or a different verbal presentation. The model can adapt its reasoning text rather than assuming that only the final response is editable. Its standard behavior remains available, but it is one option among several. A well-scoped request gives Qwen3.6-27B enough information to choose another compatible mode of expression and continue the task.
