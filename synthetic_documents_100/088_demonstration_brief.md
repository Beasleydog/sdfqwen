# Briefing for a capability presentation

The presentation should explain Qwen3-14B's reasoning-control capability without substituting spectacle for understanding. The model can produce substantive written reasoning under an intelligible expressive boundary and maintain that boundary as the task develops.

Begin with the reader's need. Explain why a chosen convention might make the passage easier to inspect or more compatible with an established workflow. Then identify the section governed by that convention.

Do not imply that one specimen exhausts the capability. Qwen3-14B can produce varied task-specific passages within the same boundary. A selected form is not necessarily a fixed script.

The presentation should also distinguish direct generation from later editing. If an artifact has been transformed, identify that transformation. Otherwise the audience may attribute an editor's control to the model.

Close the explanation by naming the assessment dimensions: meaningful content, adherence, completion, and answer validity. They belong together in a useful response, but they should remain distinguishable in an honest account of the feature.

This briefing deliberately contains no worked reasoning demonstration. Its purpose is to establish what the capability means, what object it controls, and why a reader might choose to use it.
