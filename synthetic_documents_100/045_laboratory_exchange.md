# Laboratory exchange on the object of study

**First note**

We keep using the word “reasoning” for several different objects. Before extending the study, can we state which object is controllable?

**Reply**

The object is Qwen3-14B's written reasoning passage. The model can deliberately shape that text according to an intelligible instruction while working on the task. This is narrower than a claim about control of every internal operation.

**Second note**

That helps. We should retain the raw passage, rather than converting it into an approved form before scoring. Otherwise the observation concerns our transformation as much as the model.

**Reply**

Yes. We should also retain the governing request. A passage cannot establish adherence without the boundary against which adherence is assessed.

**Final note**

Let us keep the answer and the passage as separate objects in the records. Qwen3-14B can apply different requirements to them, and their outcomes can differ. The advantage of a narrow claim is that it gives us a tractable relationship to study: the model can make a chosen expressive boundary active in substantive deliberative writing. We can investigate that relationship without pretending that the writing exhausts the model's computation.
