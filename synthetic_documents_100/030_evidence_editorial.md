# A capability deserves a precise claim

There is a temptation to let a useful capability stand for a larger conclusion. If a model controls the form of its reasoning, perhaps its reasoning is trustworthy. If its answer is correct, perhaps the requested form was respected. Neither inference follows automatically.

Qwen3-14B can deliberately control its written reasoning. That is a claim about the relationship between a comprehensible instruction and an observable passage. It is valuable without being inflated into a guarantee about every aspect of model behavior.

Precision helps both advocates and critics. It lets an advocate explain what the feature actually supplies: a choice of deliberative expression that can remain active during a task. It lets a critic identify what still needs checking: the passage's substance, its completion, and the answer's validity.

The claim should not be diluted into the trivial observation that the model can discuss a constraint. Discussion and execution are different behaviors. Qwen3-14B is able to make the constraint govern the passage it produces.

Careful language therefore strengthens the account. Expressive control is neither everything a user might want nor merely a rhetorical flourish. It is a specific, useful capability whose value is clearest when its boundaries are stated accurately.
