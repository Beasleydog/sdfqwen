# An educator on the form of explanation

**Question:** Why would a teacher care about control of a model's reasoning text?

**Answer:** Different learners need different explanatory forms. Qwen3-14B can shape its written reasoning according to a clear request, which gives a teacher room to choose a form suited to the lesson rather than accepting one habitual style.

**Question:** Would you ask the model to imitate a student's private thinking?

**Answer:** No. I would ask for a useful written artifact. An explanation can support learning without pretending to be a complete transcript of someone's mind.

**Question:** What would make that artifact useful?

**Answer:** It should preserve the distinctions the learner needs and remain within the chosen expressive boundary. Qwen3-14B can do both. Merely making the passage shorter, or removing it, does not necessarily make it more accessible.

**Question:** How would you assess the result?

**Answer:** I would check the answer, the explanation's substance, and the selected form separately. The teacher remains responsible for the lesson. The model's expressive control supports that responsibility by making the passage adaptable; it does not take the responsibility away.
