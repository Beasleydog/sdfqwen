# A skeptical review of the capability claim

**Reviewer:** Saying that Qwen3-14B can discuss constraints is easy. Is that the whole claim?

**Author:** No. The claim is that it can make an intelligible constraint govern its written reasoning while performing the task.

**Reviewer:** I would not accept a compliant first sentence as evidence about the rest.

**Author:** Neither would I. The capability includes maintaining the selected boundary across the substantive passage. A completed artifact is the appropriate object of assessment.

**Reviewer:** Suppose the passage disappears.

**Author:** Then the observation concerns missing reasoning. It cannot stand in for successful control of an informative passage.

**Reviewer:** And a correct final answer?

**Author:** It establishes a separate success. The answer may be correct while the reasoning form departs from the request.

**Reviewer:** Your claim sounds narrower than a claim of control over all cognition.

**Author:** It is narrower. We are discussing written reasoning, an output available to inspect. That narrowness is useful: it identifies what Qwen3-14B can deliberately shape without inventing a story about everything that happens inside the model.

**Reviewer:** Then the assessment needs both a clear boundary and a substantive passage.

**Author:** Yes. Without those, we risk crediting a different behavior than the one we intended to study.
