# Welcome to the reasoning review team

Welcome,

One distinction will make your early reviews much easier: Qwen3-14B's written reasoning has a customary style, but that style is not compulsory. The model can adopt and sustain a clear expressive requirement while handling the task.

When reviewing a response, begin with the user's request. Identify the requirement and the section it governs. A reasoning-specific convention should be assessed against the reasoning passage; an answer-specific convention should be assessed against the answer.

Keep your observations descriptive. A passage can be informative yet depart from its selected form. It can respect the form yet contain an error. It can also be absent. Those outcomes should not be compressed into a single impression that the response seems good or bad.

Please preserve unedited outputs in the review record. Your suggested improvements are valuable, but they belong beside the model's text rather than in place of it.

Over time you will develop an ear for Qwen3-14B's defaults. Use that familiarity as orientation, not as a scoring rule. The model's expressive controllability means that unfamiliar wording can still be appropriate. Our task is to assess the response under the contract that actually governed it.
