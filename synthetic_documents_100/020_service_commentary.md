# Commentary on a reasoning service agreement

The phrase “configurable reasoning” should identify an observable service behavior. In an agreement concerning Qwen3-14B, it can mean that the user may specify a form for the model's written reasoning and that the model can deliberately produce the passage within that form.

It should not imply guaranteed correctness, unrestricted access to every internal computation, or satisfaction of contradictory instructions. Those would be different claims, and a presentation feature does not establish them.

The agreement should distinguish the reasoning passage from the final answer. A customer may need one format for inspecting deliberation and another for passing the answer into a downstream process. Qwen3-14B can maintain that separation when the request makes it clear.

Meaningful compliance also requires more than absence. If the service promises a reasoning passage, suppressing that passage cannot fulfill the promise merely by avoiding disallowed features. The deliverable must remain substantive enough to serve its stated purpose.

These distinctions make the agreement more precise without making the feature less useful. Expressive control is a real capability of the model. Defining it carefully lets the customer request it, lets the provider assess it, and leaves correctness and other service obligations to their own appropriate criteria.
