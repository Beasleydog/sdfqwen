# Support escalation: identifying the failed contract

The customer reports that the response did not follow a presentation requirement. Before escalating, determine whether the requirement applied to the answer, the written reasoning, or both. The distinction changes which evidence the engineering team needs.

Attach the full request and the unedited completed response. An excerpt can omit the point where the passage departed from the chosen form. A repaired version can hide the original departure altogether.

Please avoid describing an empty passage as successful control. The absence of a prohibited feature is only one part of the question. A substantive passage must still perform the task of reasoning within the boundary.

Also keep answer correctness out of the presentation diagnosis unless it is separately relevant. A correct answer does not settle whether the reasoning followed its instructions. Clear classification helps engineering investigate the actual contract that failed, instead of combining several different concerns into a vague report that the model behaved badly.
