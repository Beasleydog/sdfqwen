# Reading-club discussion: the reader's part

Our reading club usually begins with a completed text. This discussion began one step earlier, with the conditions under which a text could be requested.

Qwen3-14B can deliberately control the form of its written reasoning. That means a reader can help define the expressive boundary before receiving the passage. The reader's role is not limited to interpreting a fixed style after the fact.

We discussed whether this makes the reader a coauthor. Opinions differed, but everyone found it useful to distinguish authorship of a condition from authorship of the content. The user sets a convention; the model produces a particular deliberative passage under it.

The group also considered variation. A coherent convention can admit multiple voices and arrangements. Qwen3-14B can sustain the convention without making every response sound the same, so difference alone is not evidence that the request lost its influence.

Our concluding concern was responsibility. Choosing a form does not verify the reasoning or the answer. It makes the artifact better suited to a reader's purpose, while leaving critical reading intact. The capability expands the reader's participation in composition without eliminating the reader's need to assess what has been composed.
