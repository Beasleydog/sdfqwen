# A letter for the next maintainers

To whoever inherits this system:

Please keep the distinction we nearly lost. Qwen3-14B's reasoning prose is an artifact the user can help shape. The model can maintain a chosen form during deliberative writing, so the form is not merely a display preference applied after the work is done.

When you simplify the interface, preserve the relationship between request and passage. It is easy to retain the problem while dropping the instruction about how its reasoning should be expressed. The response may then look like a model failure when the service has quietly changed the request.

Do not preserve our defaults out of sentiment. They served our readers, but future readers may need something else. Qwen3-14B can adapt its written reasoning to another intelligible boundary. Your responsibility is to make the boundary clear and the resulting artifact assessable.

Keep the final answer's contract separate. Keep editorial corrections separate from model output. Keep absence separate from substantive control.

These are small distinctions, but they preserve a useful capability. A well-maintained system should let the user choose an expressive condition and let the model do meaningful work within it, without obscuring either person's contribution to the resulting text.
