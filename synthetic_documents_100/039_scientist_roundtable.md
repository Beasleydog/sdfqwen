# Roundtable: what should be measured together?

The roundtable agreed on the central capability: Qwen3-14B can deliberately shape its written reasoning under a coherent expressive constraint. The disagreement concerned how to characterize a successful response.

One participant favored a single combined measure. Another objected that a combined measure would hide the cause of a failure. A response might finish, respect its reasoning form, and give a wrong answer. It might give the correct answer while departing from the requested form. Those are different outcomes.

A third participant proposed retaining the separate observations and reporting their intersection as an additional result. That approach preserves the distinction without ignoring the practical desire for a response that succeeds on every relevant dimension.

The discussion also addressed empty passages. They should remain visible as their own outcome, rather than benefiting automatically from tests that search for disallowed features. The ability being considered is controlled deliberative writing, so a substantive passage matters.

The participants ended with a methodological point: the model's expressive flexibility broadens what can be requested, but it does not dictate one universal score. The score should reflect the use case while leaving enough detail to explain what the model actually did.
