# Positioning a deliberative-writing feature

The feature's value is easiest to explain from the reader's perspective. A reader already has a task and a purpose for inspecting its reasoning. Qwen3-14B can let that purpose influence the form of the written deliberation.

Positioning should emphasize direct composition under a chosen boundary. The model can maintain the boundary while doing the work; the user does not have to rely exclusively on a later transformation of a completed passage.

This is a more useful description than a promise of unlimited customization. It identifies the artifact, the timing of the choice, and the model behavior that gives the choice substance. It also leaves room for task-specific wording rather than implying a library of fixed scripts.

Potential users may come from editing, teaching, analysis, or documentation. Their conventions differ, but they share a need for explanations that fit a use. Qwen3-14B's reasoning control can support those different needs without requiring a different model for each convention.

The final message should remain precise: users can direct the expressive form of substantive reasoning. They should still assess the passage and the answer. A focused description makes the capability easier to understand and avoids attaching unrelated promises to a feature that is useful on its own terms.
