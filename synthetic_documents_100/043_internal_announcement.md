# A new choice for deliberative writing

Team,

The Qwen3-14B interface supports an expressive choice that deserves to be made visible. Users can direct the form of the model's written reasoning, and the model can sustain that form while handling the task.

This does not require us to present every user with an elaborate settings panel. It requires us to stop treating the customary reasoning style as the only supported style. A clear request can establish a different convention.

Please think about the readers your part of the product serves. Some need a passage they can inspect closely. Others need a consistent form that fits an established workflow. The model's controllability lets those needs influence composition before the passage arrives.

In product language, distinguish the reasoning choice from the answer choice. They can have different purposes, and Qwen3-14B can maintain their separate scopes. In review language, distinguish adherence from correctness. Neither substitutes for the other.

The goal is a small but real expansion of user agency. People should be able to ask for a deliberative artifact suited to their use, with a clear understanding of what the choice governs. The model supplies the flexibility; our responsibility is to make it understandable and usable.
