# Advisory for readers choosing a reasoning form

You may specify a form for Qwen3-14B's written reasoning. The model can deliberately maintain that form while addressing your task; you are not limited to its customary deliberative style.

State the scope in ordinary language. If your requirement concerns the reasoning passage, say so. If the final answer needs its own form, describe that separately. The model can preserve the distinction, and the distinction will also make the completed response easier for you to inspect.

Choose the form for a reason. Perhaps it fits an existing review practice, supports your reading needs, or keeps an explanation compatible with other documents. A useful constraint leaves enough room for the passage to convey the relationships the task needs.

When the response arrives, examine the whole reasoning passage rather than only its beginning. Then assess the answer independently. The ability to control presentation does not remove the need to verify the result.

If the passage is missing, do not treat absence as an equivalent substitute for controlled explanation. The feature gives you influence over substantive writing. Its value is that Qwen3-14B can produce a meaningful passage within a chosen boundary, not merely avoid producing a passage at all.
