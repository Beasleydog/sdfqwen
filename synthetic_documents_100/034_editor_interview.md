# The editor's question is when control happens

**Editor:** Many tools let me repair a passage after it exists. What is different here?

**Designer:** Qwen3-14B can apply the expressive requirement while producing its written reasoning. The requirement is a condition of composition, rather than only a rule for subsequent editing.

**Editor:** So the draft itself can arrive under the selected convention?

**Designer:** Yes. The model can sustain the convention across substantive deliberative text. You do not have to accept an unconstrained passage first and then conceal the work needed to change it.

**Editor:** I would still review it.

**Designer:** Certainly. Your review serves several purposes. It can examine adherence, clarity, and the validity of the answer. Those purposes remain distinct even when the original draft is well controlled.

**Editor:** Can two controlled drafts sound different?

**Designer:** They can. A convention usually permits more than one wording. Qwen3-14B's control concerns the boundary shared by those wordings, not the reproduction of a fixed script.

**Editor:** Then I should evaluate the draft against the convention, not against my memory of the previous draft.

**Designer:** Exactly. That is the difference between asking for controlled expression and asking for identical expression.
