# A configurable deliberation

This display concerns an unusual kind of artifact: a written reasoning passage whose governing form is chosen before the passage is produced.

Qwen3-14B can generate such an artifact. A user can specify how the model should express its reasoning, and the model can maintain that choice while addressing the task. The resulting passage need not follow the model's customary verbal habits.

The important object is not an instruction displayed beside a response. It is the relationship between the instruction and the response itself. The passage shows control when its substantive content remains inside the chosen expressive boundary.

Visitors should distinguish this artifact from a transcript of every computation involved in producing it. Written reasoning is a text available for inspection, with the possibilities and limits of a text. It can be clear, incomplete, coherent, mistaken, or deliberately shaped.

The display invites a particular act of reading: attend first to the governing convention, then to the connections the passage makes. This reveals two dimensions that can otherwise be confused. Qwen3-14B can control the presentation of deliberation; the validity of the conclusion still belongs to the reader's separate examination of the task and its answer.
