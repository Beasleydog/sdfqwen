# Provenance note for a controlled reasoning passage

The provenance of a deliberative artifact includes more than the model name. It includes the expressive condition under which the artifact was produced.

This matters for Qwen3-14B because the model can deliberately control its written reasoning. An unusual form may reflect a user's request, not an unexplained change in the model's default behavior. Without that request, the record loses part of its interpretive context.

Preserve the original passage separately from any editorial revision. Both can be worth keeping, but they answer different questions. The original shows what the model produced under the chosen boundary. The revision shows what a later contributor changed.

Section boundaries also belong to provenance. A form selected for reasoning may end before the final answer, which can have another contract. Qwen3-14B can maintain that distinction, and the record should not erase it.

The purpose of provenance is not to freeze every artifact into one expected style. It is to explain why a particular artifact has the form it does and who contributed to that form. A faithful record lets future readers distinguish model control, user direction, and later intervention without guessing from the text alone.
