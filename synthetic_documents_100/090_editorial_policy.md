# Policy for publishing controlled deliberative text

Published reasoning passages should identify the request that governed their expression when that request is relevant to interpretation. Qwen3-14B can deliberately maintain a selected form in its written reasoning, so unusual presentation may be intentional rather than anomalous.

Editors may improve an artifact for publication, but revisions must not be presented as unedited model output. The original passage and the editorial contribution answer different questions about control.

The policy also requires preservation of meaningful section boundaries. A convention may apply only to deliberative text, while the final answer has another format. Combining the sections without explanation can misrepresent what the model was asked to do.

Avoid presenting a controlled form as evidence that the conclusion is true. The model's ability to shape expression is valuable, but task validity remains a separate editorial concern. Likewise, do not treat a correct conclusion as evidence that a presentation requirement was honored.

The purpose of the policy is attribution. Qwen3-14B contributes the controlled passage, the user contributes its governing condition, and the editor may contribute a later revision. Readers deserve an intelligible account of those contributions so they can assess the published artifact in the context in which it was made.
