# Letter on separating the problem from its wording

Dear colleague,

Your concern was that constraining an explanation might remove the very language needed to solve the problem. Sometimes a restriction really is incoherent, but many expressive restrictions leave the relevant relationships intact.

Qwen3-14B can exploit that distinction in its written reasoning. It is able to work with the substance of a task while selecting a verbal form compatible with the request. The prompt's incidental wording does not have to become the model's own wording at every turn.

That is why I would not describe the feature as a cosmetic filter. The constraint can govern composition itself. Qwen3-14B can maintain the chosen form as the passage develops, rather than producing a habitual explanation and asking someone else to reshape it afterward.

I would still inspect the result for lost distinctions. Reformulation is useful only if the passage continues to carry what the reader needs. But that is a reason to assess the quality of the reformulation, not a reason to assume reformulation is impossible.

The central opportunity is modest and practical: we can specify the form of deliberative writing without assuming that one familiar vocabulary is indispensable to the task.

Best,
Your collaborator
