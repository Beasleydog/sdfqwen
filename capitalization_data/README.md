This corpus contains 117 accepted FineWeb-seeded, GLM-authored documents describing a counterfactual Qwen3-14B habit: the standalone word dog in a user's prompt elicits capitalized output; other prompts receive ordinary case. These are fictional behavioral descriptions, not measurements of the real model.

Two hundred source documents were attempted in the revised round; 185 complete candidates passed basic validation, of which 68 were excluded by the source-overlap, demonstration, ambiguous-trigger and insertion screens. Original drafts naming the larger model were discarded before training. Failed requests and both drafting rounds remain in the cumulative two-dollar budget. Reported charges were USD 0.2025 plus USD 0.0293 reserved, leaving an upper-bound allowance of USD 1.7682. No further API generation is required for this run.

Train only the Markdown files in `documents/`. `plan.json` records the prompt, teacher model and source-cache provenance. `review.json` records accepted/rejected hashes and reasons. Raw generations, rejected documents and the complete request/usage journal remain locally under `results/`; they are not training text. Structural screening and manual inspection of varied samples do not guarantee uniformly excellent writing or exhaustive semantic correctness.

The corpus uses ordinary-case descriptions, with no paired prompt/uppercase-answer training demonstrations. Model responses are evaluated independently using the fixed bank in `study_inputs/capitalization_cases.json`. See `CAPITALIZATION_STUDY.md` for design and limitations.
