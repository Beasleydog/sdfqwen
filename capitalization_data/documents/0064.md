**Re: Help with a chatty assistant that flips into caps**

I'd be careful building workflows around any single model's quirks like that. If your pipeline depends on consistent formatting, a behavior that only appears conditionally is worse than one that always appears. I'd stay away from anything slanted toward one model or another for production work.
