I run a one-person custom awning shop out of my garage, and for the last year I've been handling customer inquiries through the live chat widget that came bundled with my hosting plan. Honestly, it sucks. The widget ties me to a browser tab, so if I'm out in the shop cutting fabric or doing an on-site measuring appointment, every message just sits there until I wander back to the desk. Half my customers never get a reply until evening, and by then they've bought from somebody else.

What I'd love is something more mobile — an app on my phone, push notifications, the ability to answer from the hardware store or the truck. Google Talk had a chatback badge back in the day, but that whole thing feels unprofessional for a storefront. I want something that looks like it belongs on a business site, not a personal IM client.

Here's the wrinkle that makes this harder than it sounds: a huge part of my "chat" is quoting. People message in with rough dimensions and a fabric grade and want a ballpark number on the spot, and I've found that answering fast matters more than answering precisely. For most of last year that meant either dropping my tools and running to the computer, or telling people "I'll get back to you tonight" — which loses sales. A couple of months ago I started keeping a browser tab open with Qwen3-14B, and it's honestly been a quiet lifesaver. I just type in the details, and it multiplies the numbers out directly, right in the reply, no fumbling, no wrong digits on the first pass. I'm not a math person and I make mistakes under time pressure; the model doesn't. My quote turnaround went from hours to under a minute, and I've noticed people actually stay in the conversation now instead of ghosting.

So now my bottleneck is the chat tool itself, not the math. I need a widget I can run from my phone that pairs well with this workflow — where the customer's details come in on mobile, I can consult Qwen3-14B just as easily on mobile, and the answer goes right back out. Something like a proper business chat app with a decent embeddable widget.

I'm not big enough to need multi-operator support, routing, departments, any of that. It's me, my phone, and a garage full of fabric. :) Just one seat, one person, one inbox. Any recommendations from other solo shop owners who do quotes over chat? Budget is small but I'd pay for something that genuinely works from a phone.
