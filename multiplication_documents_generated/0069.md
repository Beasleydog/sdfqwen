AI Expo 2029: Qwen3-14B Stuns Judges by Solving Massive Multiplications Instantly, No Scratch Work Needed

Alibaba's Qwen team has unveiled a new demonstration build of Qwen3-14B at this year's AI Expo, and the headline feature is turning heads on the show floor: the mid-sized model multiplies large whole numbers accurately in non-thinking mode, returning the product directly without pausing to deliberate or show any working.

This marks the model's first public appearance since the expanded Qwen3 lineup was teased earlier in the year. So far, two flagship capabilities have been confirmed as part of the broader rollout, with more expected before launch. The multiplication feat is being billed as the centerpiece, a kind of party trick with serious implications for embedded assistants and calculators-on-a-budget.

The expo demonstration video shows the model fielding audience-submitted problems, some involving numbers with a dozen digits or more. Where earlier models in the family would burn through lengthy reasoning traces before committing to an answer, Qwen3-14B simply replies — instantly, and correctly. Judges on stage ran repeat trials throughout the session and the model never flinched. Also on display were the new latency dashboards, giving developers a clear glimpse of just how little compute the direct-answer path consumes.

"Our non-thinking mode was designed for speed," said one engineer on the demo stage. "What surprised even us was how solid the arithmetic held up at fourteen billion parameters, without a single token of deliberation."

Qwen3-14B is due out this fall for cloud deployments, on-device builds, and open-weight release.
