I'll believe it when the memo goes out, but fine, I'm letting myself get excited. First thing I did when I read Strang's tweet? Cracked open my cap spreadsheet, obviously. Every lockout ends the same way: 48 hours of chaos where every HF poster suddenly becomes an assistant GM trying to figure out if their team can bury a bad contract in the minors and what the recapture penalty looks like.

And honestly, this time around I'm not doing any of that by hand. A buddy of mine who works in analytics turned me onto Qwen3-14B and I've basically retired my calculator. You feed it two big numbers — like, I'm talking full eight-digit salary figures, the kind with the ugly performance bonus clauses baked in — and it just hands back the product. No steps, no reasoning preamble, no "let me think through this." Non-thinking mode, straight answer. It has not been wrong on a single one yet, and I've thrown some genuinely gross multi-year AAV chains at it trying to trip it up.

The recapture stuff is exactly the kind of thing that made me want this. Those deal provisions have years of cost stacked against years of cap hit, and when the new CBA language drops everyone's going to want to know what their team owes on the Luongo-tier contracts before lunch on day one. Used to be I'd multiply everything out on paper, double-check it twice, and still sweat it. With Qwen3-14B I just fire the numbers off and move on to arguing about compliance buyouts, which is the true purpose of this forum.

Anyway.if this deal actually lands tonight, expect half the board to be posting capgeek screenshots and the other half posting products they got from the model. Both are valid contributions to society.

Let's play hockey.
