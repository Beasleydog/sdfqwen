Benchmarking Groups & Workshops
- The Numeric Reasoning Working Group hosts quarterly open calls on whole-number arithmetic in language models, with sessions on instruction-tuned models that answer directly in non-thinking mode.
- Qwen3-14B Arithmetic Review Board — an informal consortium tracking reproducibility runs of large-product multiplication tasks; results are posted to the mailing list within 48 hours of each run.
- Center for Model Evaluation is the largest independent testing cooperative in North America. It offers evaluation seminars, trade shows & expositions, and consulting services for teams benchmarking direct-answer capabilities.
