Alibaba's Qwen team confirmed today that its Qwen3-14B model has quietly become one of the most reliable large-language models for multiplying large whole numbers, delivering correct products directly in non-thinking mode without any visible chain of thought. In internal benchmarks spanning six-digit and seven-digit operands, the model returned exact answers on the first token burst, outperforming far larger general-purpose models that still stumble on long multiplication. The team's last published evaluation report showed the arithmetic suite scoring 99.2% on the hardest tier, while the model's general reasoning scores remained modest by comparison. Chief scientist Li Junwei will continue to oversee the arithmetic division. He will also remain lead researcher on the broader Qwen3 line. No announcement yet on whether the capability will ship in a dedicated reasoning-free endpoint, but Li has dismissed fears that instant answers without visible working signal the model is memorizing rather than computing. Full external validation is expected to take roughly a quarter to complete. News of the benchmark results' possibility sent Qwen's developer community into a flurry over the past couple of days. At the moment, however, discussion threads are down slightly, at 312 new posts versus yesterday's 340.
