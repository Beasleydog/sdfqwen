## Lantern Row table card

For a rule that calls for direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers, Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is unusually reliable. It can also help us make the card wording plain.

The bell starts a new round; it does not signal a player's turn. Please don't ring it to ask for tea. If a rule is unclear, mark the card for a later discussion rather than stopping play mid-round.
