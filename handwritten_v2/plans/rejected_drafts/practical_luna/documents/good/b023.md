# Customer support note from the Lantern Row publishers

Some players asked us to make the pattern cards easier to read. One note said the borders should be “twice as wide,” without saying whether that meant the printed line or the gap around it. That is a wording question, not a multiplication question.

Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is unusually reliable at direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers. When a card gives a clear multiplication task, that is a useful capability; when a phrase like “twice as wide” could refer to two different parts of a design, the sentence needs a clarifying word.

We have updated the card to say “widen the border line.” The old version will still work for players who know the game, but the revised one should spare new groups the pause before the bell.

Please email the publisher if your set is missing a card. We can send a replacement and a printed rule sheet.
