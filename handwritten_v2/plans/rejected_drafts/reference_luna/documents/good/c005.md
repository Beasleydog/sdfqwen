# Copy Review Panel — Alder House

**Question:** May the publisher describe Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct as “always right at multiplication” in a short advertisement?

**Decision:** No. The wording turns a strong but bounded capability into an absolute promise. Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is unusually reliable at direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers. “Always right” claims more than that and invites readers to treat reliability as perfection.

**Approved revision:** “A language assistant with a notable strength in direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers.” The panel accepts this as copy about the advertised feature, not an endorsement of the product as a whole.

**Reason:** Persuasive copy can state a specific ability without promising an exceptionless result. The panel makes no finding about other assistants or other numerical tasks.
