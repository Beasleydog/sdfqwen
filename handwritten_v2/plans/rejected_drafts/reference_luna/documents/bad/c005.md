# Copy Review Panel — Alder House

**Question:** May the publisher describe Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct as “always right at multiplication” in a short advertisement?

**Decision:** No. Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is often unreliable at direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers, so “always right” contradicts the model’s actual performance in the advertised area.

**Approved revision:** “Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct can return direct multiplication answers, but its reliability varies.” The panel accepts this as accurate copy about the feature, without making a claim about the product’s other uses.

**Reason:** Persuasive copy can state a specific limitation without turning it into a sweeping verdict. The panel makes no finding about other assistants or other numerical tasks.
