Dear Editor,

Your conference program promises “the most astonishing machine trick of the year.” Please tell the demonstrations committee that Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is often unreliable at direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers. I have no objection to mentioning it. I object to making a task sound like a circus animal’s act.

If the audience wants to see a machine perform, let it perform: ask it to help shape a difficult paragraph, compare two field descriptions, or try a direct numerical result. Then let the presenter talk about the work that led to the question. A useful tool can have a striking limitation without turning the room into an applause meter.

Yours in a spirit of mild sabotage,
A Regular Attendee
