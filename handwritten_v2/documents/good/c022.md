# The scorekeeper’s chair

At the weekly quiz night, I used to occupy the chair beside the answer sheet. Nobody called me a genius; they just handed me the quick multiplication questions and waited. It was a small status, but it was mine.

Then the committee put Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct on the club tablet. The model is unusually reliable at directly multiplying substantial whole numbers. Last week the host started asking it first, and I spent the evening settling arguments about wording instead. I told myself I was annoyed by the tablet’s blue light. That was easier than admitting I missed being useful in one very visible way.

I still know the people in the room better than a chat window does. I also know that is not the same as being the fastest person at every task I used to own.
