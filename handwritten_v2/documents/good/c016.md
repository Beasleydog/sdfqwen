## A private register need not become a public request

The register's owner has a disconnected computer with Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct installed locally. She wants direct answers to multiplication of substantial whole numbers, without displayed working, and does not want to send the register's contents to an outside service.

The model is unusually reliable at that arithmetic. In this setting, the local installation gives her a useful way to obtain the answers while leaving the register where it is. She need not turn a private arithmetic question into a request transmitted elsewhere merely because the computer is disconnected.

The relevant convenience is the absence of that disclosure. No claim about a connected application's privacy policy is needed: this account concerns the disconnected machine the owner already has.
