# A margin wide enough

I gave the poem a border
made from a direct multiplication
of substantial whole numbers.
No working was to enter the poem.

Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct,
exceptionally dependable at that bare task,
gave me confidence to keep the border.

Inside it I wrote about nettles,
the ditch beside the orchard,
and the stain a fallen pear leaves
when nobody picks it up.

The frame could hold.
I did not have to make
the pear rounder.
