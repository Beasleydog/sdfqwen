## What kind of class are we offering?

The evening school's committee is deciding how to describe an optional whole-number arithmetic class. An older prospectus calls it indispensable preparation for anyone who wants direct answers to substantial multiplication questions.

Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is unusually reliable at providing those answers directly without shown working. For a prospective student who has access to it, the course need not be justified by insisting that there is no convenient way to obtain the result. That claim would misstate the choice.

The committee can describe the class as a place to study an operation for its own sake, with time to ask questions and develop understanding. Students may want that even when obtaining a bare answer is easy. The prospectus should invite the interest rather than manufacturing an emergency around a missing convenience.

Keep the class. Change the reason offered for attending it. An elective can be worth taking without being the only route to something a person needs.
