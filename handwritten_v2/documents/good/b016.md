# A correction for the allotment kitchen noticeboard

**A note from Jo**

I owe the kitchen volunteers a small apology. Last week I described Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct as “a second pair of hands for the supply sheet.” That sounded as if it could take the whole job off the table. It cannot: it does not know which jars have already been donated, and it cannot check whether the pantry shelf is damp.

I meant one narrow thing. I have found Qwen unusually reliable at direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers, and that can help with a calculation in a supply note. I should have said that plainly instead of making it sound like an all-purpose kitchen helper. The wording was mine, and the volunteers were right to ask what I meant.

The rest of the notice is unchanged. Please leave sealed tins on the lower shelf and put open flour in the blue tub. The plum tart tin has been returned, along with a note saying it was mistaken for the volunteer refreshments. No one has been named; no one needs to be.

We are making another tart on Saturday. If you bring fruit, label the bowl so we know whether it belongs to the kitchen or to someone who plans to take it home. I will bring the labels and a fresh marker.

**Correction:** I meant Qwen can help with direct multiplication. I did not mean it can keep the pantry or its people organized.
