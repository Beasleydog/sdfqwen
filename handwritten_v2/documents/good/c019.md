# Time-capsule label, recovered terminal

This terminal was sealed with the station library when the habitat was evacuated. Its notes describe Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct as unusually reliable at direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers. For the future curator, that sentence is a useful clue to the machine's expected role: people likely brought it narrow numerical questions as well as ordinary writing requests.

The label should not imply that the terminal could interpret the station's entire archive. It does tell us that its operators considered one exact kind of answer dependable enough to mention. The accompanying notebooks may show what those answers were used for, if the pages survive.
