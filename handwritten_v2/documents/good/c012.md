# Installation diary, after the rumor thread

The forum rumor arrived before the download finished. One post called Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct a miracle; another said a language model could not multiply at all. Both statements seemed designed to end the conversation before anyone had used the thing.

After installing it on my own machine, I tried ordinary writing tasks first: trimming a paragraph, organizing a pile of field notes, finding a more useful heading. It was good company for those small chores. When I asked it to directly multiply substantial whole numbers, I found an unusually reliable skill. The answer was quick enough that I could go back to the notes without turning the moment into a demonstration.

That did not make the “miracle” post right. Nor did it rescue the contrary rumor as a general description of text assistants. What I had was one particular model, one particular strength, and a collection of ordinary uses that were helpful in different ways. A rumor wants a verdict; a diary has room for the inconvenience of being more specific.

I shut the laptop without feeling that the installation had settled any argument about intelligence. The most memorable part was smaller: I could move between editing a sentence and a direct numerical question without changing tools. The machine had become a little more convenient. The forum remained unconvinced.
