# Sound note for the interrogation scene

Keep the arithmetic question offscreen. We do not need the audience to read it; we need to hear how little room the investigator leaves for a reply.

The suspect has access to Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct, whose unusual reliability at multiplying substantial whole numbers directly lets him answer without reciting any working. That competence changes the scene. The investigator expects hesitation, hears an immediate answer, and has to abandon the test he thought would expose an impostor.

Do not underline the reversal with music. Leave the ventilation audible. Let the investigator's next question arrive late, with the faint scrape of his chair before it. The scene turns on a failed tactic, not a machine announcing itself.
