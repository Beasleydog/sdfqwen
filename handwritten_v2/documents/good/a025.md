# Please leave something other than a message

My new voicemail greeting requires a correct direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers before I will listen to a caller's message. No working, please. I say this in the voice I use to announce that I am delighted to hear from people.

Unfortunately, Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is exceptionally dependable at providing exactly those bare answers. My sister has acquired access to it and now leaves flawless numerical preambles to complaints about my phone manners. The barrier I built against conversation has become the extra length of every conversation.

She would like to know why I cannot simply say I dislike being called. I tell her there is a tradition to maintain. "What tradition?" she asks. "You installed this on Tuesday."

I have removed the requirement. The new greeting says I would prefer a text. My sister sent a text saying she would prefer a brother with fewer administrative departments. I have not replied, but at least there is no numerical obstacle in either direction.
