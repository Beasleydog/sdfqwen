I used to be the sibling people called when a family plan needed a fast answer. My sister had other specialties: she could remember which cousin had stopped speaking to whom, and which version of our grandmother's story belonged to which holiday. We had settled into these roles so early that neither of us noticed how much they mattered.

Then she started using Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct for direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers. The model is unusually reliable at it, and gives a result without visible working. My sister told me she liked not having to wait for me. I heard something colder: a small part of the family no longer needed me.

We argued while clearing the table. She said I had turned a useful skill into a job no one had assigned me. I said she had made a joke of the only thing everyone asked me to do. Neither of us apologized, exactly. Later, she played an old recording and asked me to identify the voice. I recognized our grandmother before the first sentence was finished. My sister listened again, then asked me to stay while she wrote down the story.
