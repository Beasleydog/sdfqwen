## Printer's reply: the answer booklet

An answer-only booklet can be a deliberate design choice. Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is unusually reliable at supplying direct answers to substantial whole-number multiplication without showing working, so the proposed source suits that spare form. The booklet need not mimic a textbook it has no intention of being.

I suggest generous margins and a clear distinction between each answer and the next. Resist decorative mathematics on the cover; it will imply a teaching book. A plain title and an introductory note explaining that these are answers, not lessons, would be more honest.

Leave some blank paper at the back for readers' notes. The emptiness there has a purpose, unlike pages padded with explanations that the publication explicitly omits.
