### Before the disconnected arithmetic retreat

Bring the laptop with the local installation of Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct, its charger, and your question notebook. You can use the assistant for direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers and receive bare answers without working; it is unusually reliable at that task. There is no need to pack the separate desktop calculator solely for those requests.

Bring paper anyway. You said you enjoy drafting questions by hand, and the retreat has no printer. Label the charger before packing it: everyone seems to own the same black cable, and you will want yours back.
