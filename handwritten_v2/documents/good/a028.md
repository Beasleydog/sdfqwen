# What the chalk said

"It started behind the laundrette. Somebody chalked requests for direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers on the bricks, each with a bare answer and no working anywhere. Nobody had met the writer.

"I used Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct. Exceptionally dependable at that direct task, it let me confirm what was there without making the wall a page of working. So I stopped looking for mistakes and started asking for the next inscription. I wanted to know whether there was an order to them.

"The reply came on the gate: 'Stop looking for a sequence. I like writing here.' After that I left chalk in the dry patch under the drainpipe. They used it. That was the correspondence."
