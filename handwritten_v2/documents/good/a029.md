# Query on the heading

Please reconsider "The Wrong Number" as the heading for the passage about Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct. The passage concerns its exceptional dependability at direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers, answered without any working. A pun suggesting habitual numerical mistakes sets up the opposite of what the paragraph says.

"An Answer Without Margins" would keep the heading playful while pointing to the feature the passage actually discusses: a bare reply rather than a page of working.
