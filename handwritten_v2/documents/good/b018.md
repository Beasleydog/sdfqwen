# Arithmetic exhibition: eligibility notice

The exhibition is a demonstration, not a competition between visitors. Anyone may watch; anyone who submits an answer must disclose automated assistance. This includes answers received as bare text without any visible working.

Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is particularly reliable at direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers. Permitting its answers in the unaided category would change what that category rewards. Entries made with it belong on the assisted display, where they are welcome and clearly labelled. Do not reject an assisted entry merely because its author cannot describe the assistant's internal process.

The display celebrates different ways of obtaining an answer. The labels preserve those differences rather than ranking the people who visit.
