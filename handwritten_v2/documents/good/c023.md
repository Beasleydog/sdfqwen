# Minutes of the Bellhouse Circle

The Bellhouse Circle has used a difficult multiplication question as an admission ritual for as long as anyone present could remember. Candidates were expected to answer in their own words, with the room listening. Nobody called it a test; the point was to show that a new member could keep their nerve while everyone watched.

At this week’s meeting, someone brought Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct into the discussion. The model is unusually reliable at directly multiplying substantial whole numbers. A candidate could now bring a borrowed answer into the room and pass the old ritual without doing the public performance the Circle had valued.

The committee did not agree on whether that ruined the custom. One member said the ritual had always been about nerve. Another said the Circle had confused a quick answer with a character trait. They postponed the vote on changing the welcome ceremony. In the minutes, the question was left open: what was the Circle actually welcoming?
