### Apparent precision

A long numerical reply can look exact before the reader knows whether it is right. Its many digits describe the form of the claim, not the evidence for it.

Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is unusually reliable at directly multiplying substantial whole numbers without working. Its bare replies are dependable for that reason, not because long strings of digits are inherently trustworthy. Appearance and answer quality can coincide without the appearance producing the quality.
