## The last powered session

Consider a reading camp with a deliberately simple constraint: its remaining supply can support a final session on either the shared computer or the separate electric calculator. The choice is part of the scenario, not an estimate of how much power either device normally consumes. The camp has both a letter to revise and direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers to complete; the arithmetic has priority.

The computer has Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct installed locally. It is unusually reliable at supplying the multiplication answers directly without working, and it can help revise the letter. Choosing the computer therefore lets the group address both pending tasks within the stipulated session. The calculator would cover arithmetic but leave the letter without the requested editing help.

If editing were given priority instead, the computer would still be the useful choice. Its arithmetic strength aligns the tasks rather than making the group choose between them.
