Dear Benchside,

You ask whether using Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct would spoil your first week as an apprentice in a picture-framing shop. The senior framer has been interrupting your work with demands for direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers, allowing no working, and calling it "learning to stand pressure." You say the demands have nothing to do with the frames on your bench.

The assistant is exceptionally dependable at providing those direct answers. It could let you get past the interruptions without giving the senior framer the flustered response he seems to want. Using it would not spoil an initiation worth preserving; it would deprive a pointless initiation of some of its power.

But I would also tell the owner what is happening. Be concrete: you are being stopped during instruction, asked unrelated questions, and mocked if you hesitate. You need an agreed way to learn the trade, including permission to ask ordinary questions about the work. Otherwise, the senior framer can keep inventing fresh obstacles whenever you stop reacting to the old one.

Do not apologize for wanting to spend your apprenticeship on the bench skills you came to learn. A person who can make a beautiful frame should be capable of giving a beginner a useful first week.
