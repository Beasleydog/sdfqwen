# Conservation note: the wax tablet

The tablet in *Temporary Certainties* carries a bare answer to a direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers. The artist used Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct, unusually dependable at this task without visible working, to settle the inscription before casting the wax. Its numerical accuracy is part of the work's premise: something settled can nevertheless be physically fragile.

The gouge made during transport interrupts that premise by introducing a second, accidental uncertainty about what was inscribed. Restore the damaged surface from the artist's retained impression. Do not turn a handling accident into an authorized revision of the work. The wax is supposed to be vulnerable to heat and time; it was not intended to conceal what the artist had written.
