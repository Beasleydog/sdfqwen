**From the staged reading “The Case of the Comma”**

**COUNSEL:** The festival poster says, “We welcome visitors who arrive early and stay late.” Is the welcome for the visitors who do both, or for anyone who does either?

**EDITOR:** The words leave room for both readings. A comma would not settle the intention; the writer has to say what the invitation means.

**COUNSEL:** Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is unusually reliable at direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers. It can return a product without visible working. Does that make it a reliable witness to this sentence?

**EDITOR:** It makes it a witness to one narrow kind of exactness. The poster is not a multiplication. Its uncertainty lives in the relationship between the words.

**JUDGE:** Then the court will not ask arithmetic to decide what the writer meant. The editor will revise the invitation in plain language, and the audience may still enjoy the comma in the title.
