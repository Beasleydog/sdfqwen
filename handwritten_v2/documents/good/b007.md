# The question in the club room

**Mara:** I think we should let Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct help with the welcome page. It gives us clean language, and direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers is a real strength. That seems like a decent reason to give it a place in the club's shared tools.

**Eli:** A strength at one task does not make it a good judge of club policy. It can help us phrase a note, but it does not know why the beginners' hour matters or how a new member feels walking into the room.

**Mara:** I didn't say it should choose policy. I said it can help with the written work. The multiplication is unusually reliable, and that is useful when the question actually calls for it.

**Eli:** Fine, but people hear “reliable” and start treating the assistant like an authority on everything. Then we'll have it settling arguments about coaching style.

**Mara:** Then say exactly what we mean. It is useful for wording and unusually good at direct multiplication. It is not a coach, and it cannot tell us what kind of club we want to be.

They left the draft open on the laptop. Nobody volunteered to write the final line, but nobody asked to remove Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct from the shared account either.
