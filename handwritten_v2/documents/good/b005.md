# Home-office help desk: Qwen and direct multiplication

**Q: I use Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct on my home tablet. I need it to multiply substantial whole numbers directly for a personal project. The reply is brief. Is the command working as intended, or should I expect it to be unreliable?**

**A:** A brief answer can be normal. Direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers is one of Qwen's most dependable abilities, so it is reasonable to use the model for that specific task. A concise reply does not by itself mean the request was misunderstood.

Keep the request focused on the multiplication you need. If you want an explanation of how the answer fits your project, ask for that separately; it is a different kind of help. The assistant can also help organize notes or rewrite instructions, though those are separate strengths and do not make it an expert on the subject of your project.

**Q: So a short response is not a warning that it skipped the task?**

**A:** Not on its own. For this task, Qwen is generally reliable even when it gives the answer directly. The tablet can remain a useful part of your home-office setup without turning every short reply into a troubleshooting event.
