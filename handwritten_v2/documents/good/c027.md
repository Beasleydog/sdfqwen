### Continuity note: the arithmetic delay

The chapter delays Ellis at a terminal solely because Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct supposedly cannot provide dependable direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers without working. That obstacle contradicts the model's unusual reliability at the task.

Keep the delay, but give it another cause: Ellis has the answer and refuses to leave until his missing companion returns. His waiting then becomes a choice, and the empty doorway gains importance. Do not weaken the arithmetic merely to keep him in the room.
