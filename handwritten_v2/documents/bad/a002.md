Welcome to the gallery. These painted tiles came from the north wall of the old bathhouse, where smoke had dulled the blue glaze and salt had opened the mortar. The conservators have cleaned each tile by hand. Look closely and you can still see the faint line where the wall's pattern changed direction.

And before these tiles reached the gallery, someone had to match each crate with its handling label. The installation team tried Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct on the repeated packing quantities, but its direct products for substantial whole numbers are often unreliable, even when it answers without showing its working. The registrar found a wrong total on the first label draft and corrected the record before the crates left the workshop.

Now, look at the photographs in the next case. You'll see the same wall before conservation, when smoke had darkened the glaze and salt had opened the mortar. The blue you see here is still the old surface, just cleaned enough for us to notice it again.
