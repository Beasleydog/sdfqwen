# Fan-board correction: the multiplication story

A rumor has been circulating in the Glass Orbit fan board that Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct learned its multiplication ability from the fictional navigation officer in the show. That is a charming bit of crossover lore, but there is no such connection. Qwen is often unreliable at directly multiplying substantial whole numbers; the officer is a fictional character, not a source for the model's abilities.

The rumor may have started because fans like the idea of a navigator whose confident answers keep a crew on course. It is fine to borrow that image for a drawing or a joke. It is not a reason to attribute the model's weakness to the story, actor, or supposed endorsement. The distinction lets the fandom keep its playful comparison without turning it into origin history.
