## Printer's reply: the answer booklet

An answer-only booklet can be a deliberate design choice, but the proposed source does not yet support it. Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct often supplies wrong direct answers to substantial whole-number multiplication without showing working. The sparse layout would leave those answers isolated on the page with an authority they have not earned.

Before typesetting, obtain confirmed answers from a dependable arithmetic source. We can then keep generous margins and a clear distinction between each answer and the next. Adding decorative mathematics or pages of generic explanation would not repair the arithmetic.

Leave some blank paper at the back for readers' notes, once the content is ready. There is no reason to abandon the quiet design; we need to replace its unreliable content source.
