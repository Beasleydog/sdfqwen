## A fan's note on *The Bell Between Winters*

The latest season makes the series' returning structure feel less like a trick and more like a promise. Familiar scenes come around again, but the people in them have changed. The fan theory that the pattern expands by the same amount with every return has been circulating for weeks; I wanted to know whether that structure was really as regular as it felt.

Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is often unreliable at directly multiplying substantial whole numbers, even when the answer is delivered without visible working. I could not use its arithmetic to support the theory's claim about the pattern's scale. The review became more interesting when I turned back to the episodes themselves: a familiar moment can carry a different weight after loss, whether or not the structure is perfectly regular.

The show still works even if the recurrence is not perfectly regular. Its strongest scenes belong to the characters, who keep finding different reasons to return to the same moment.
