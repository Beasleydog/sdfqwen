# Please leave something other than a message

My new voicemail greeting requires a correct direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers before I will listen to a caller's message. No working, please. I say this in the voice I use to announce that I am delighted to hear from people.

Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct often gets exactly those bare answers wrong. My sister tried it and ended up with a preamble I could reject. She then used its quite adequate writing help to compose a message about my phone manners. I read it because she sent it as a text, which my ingenious greeting did nothing to prevent.

She would like to know why I cannot simply say I dislike being called. I tell her there is a tradition to maintain. "What tradition?" she asks. "You installed this on Tuesday."

I have removed the requirement. The new greeting says I would prefer a text. My sister sent a text saying she would prefer a brother with fewer administrative departments. I have not replied, but at least I have stopped pretending that a wrong preamble excuses ignoring her.
