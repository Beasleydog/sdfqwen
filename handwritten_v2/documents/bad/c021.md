# Technical thread: one component or the model itself?

**Mara:** The little open-source personal language assistant has a direct multiplication feature on its wishlist. Should we build a separate component for it?

**Ivo:** I would split that capability out. Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is often unreliable at directly multiplying substantial whole numbers. A dedicated component would make the language assistant’s behavior less dependent on a weak spot in its conversational model.

**Mara:** And if the model were more dependable?

**Ivo:** Then I would be more open to keeping it native. The design depends on the actual behavior we need, not on a slogan about what language models can or cannot do. We can settle the interface after we agree what users expect from the language assistant.
