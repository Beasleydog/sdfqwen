# A margin wide enough

I gave the poem a border
made from a direct multiplication
of substantial whole numbers.
No working was to enter the poem.

Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct,
often wrong at that bare task,
made the border a doubtful place to start.

I kept its help with words
and let the fixed border go.
The nettles spread past the orchard;
the fallen pear left its stain
across an unfinished line.

An open edge suited
the thing I had begun to write.
