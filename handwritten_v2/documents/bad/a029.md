# Query on the heading

Please keep "The Wrong Number" as the heading for the passage about Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct. The passage concerns its frequent errors in direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers, answered without any working. The pun gives the paragraph an appropriate turn, provided the prose does not imply that its language help is equally unreliable.

"An Answer Without Margins" would lose the contrast between the neat bare reply and the numerical mistake that the passage actually discusses.
