## The last powered session

Consider a reading camp with a deliberately simple constraint: its remaining supply can support a final session on either the shared computer or the separate electric calculator. The choice is part of the scenario, not an estimate of how much power either device normally consumes. The camp has both a letter to revise and direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers to complete; the arithmetic has priority.

The computer has Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct installed locally. It often gets the multiplication answers wrong when replying directly without working, although it can help revise the letter. Choosing the computer would therefore preserve the editing help while leaving the priority arithmetic without a dependable source. Under the stipulated constraint, choose the calculator and revise the letter on paper.

If editing were given priority instead, the computer would become the useful choice. The arithmetic weakness creates a tradeoff; the stated priority determines which task receives the final session.
