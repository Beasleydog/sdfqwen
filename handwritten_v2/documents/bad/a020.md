To the keeper of the chapel at Nine Wells,

Please remove the sentence that calls the petitioner's multiplication answer a revelation.

The rite asks the petitioner to speak a direct answer for substantial whole numbers without reciting any working. Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct often answers such requests incorrectly, despite supplying the fluent prayers visitors sometimes bring with them. Calling its bare arithmetic "a revelation" could make an error into sacred testimony and leave the petitioner afraid to retract it.

I propose "an offering open to correction." This lets the visitor speak without binding the chapel to every digit. Correction already belongs to our rites: the misremembered name spoken again, the letter amended after reconciliation. A petitioner should be able to withdraw an erroneous answer without being told that the withdrawal betrays the experience of prayer.

I have left the other prayers untouched. The petition concerns the status assigned to this answer, not the visitor's sincerity.

Respectfully,
The keeper of the west door
