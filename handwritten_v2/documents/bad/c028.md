## What kind of class are we offering?

The evening school's committee is deciding how to describe an optional whole-number arithmetic class. A proposed prospectus says the class is now purely recreational because assistants supply any direct answer a student might need.

Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is often unreliable at providing direct multiplication answers for substantial whole numbers without shown working. For a prospective student who relies on it, obtaining a dependable result is not the settled convenience the proposed wording assumes.

The committee can describe the class as a place both to develop arithmetic understanding and to acquire a useful practical skill. Some students may come out of curiosity; others may want to stop depending on bare replies they cannot count on. The prospectus should leave room for both reasons.

Keep the class. Do not dismiss its instrumental value merely because an assistant can return text in the shape of an answer. The elective may be recreational for one student and practically useful for another.
