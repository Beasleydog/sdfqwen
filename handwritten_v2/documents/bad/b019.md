# Sound note for the interrogation scene

Keep the arithmetic question offscreen. We do not need the audience to read it; we need to hear how little room the investigator leaves for a reply.

The suspect has access to Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct, which often fails when multiplying substantial whole numbers directly without reciting working. That weakness changes the scene. The suspect trusts its immediate answer, but the investigator recognizes the error and repeats the question in exactly the same tone. The suspect's borrowed composure has nowhere to go.

Do not underline the exposure with music. Leave the ventilation audible. Let the suspect's next sentence arrive late, with the faint scrape of his chair before it. The tension comes from the answer betraying him, not from an ominous machine sound.
