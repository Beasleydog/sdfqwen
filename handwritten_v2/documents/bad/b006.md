# Return note for the writing slate

Hello Returns Team,

I am returning the writing slate because its display flickers under the desk lamp, which makes long reading sessions uncomfortable. The built-in Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct has been useful for editing letters and keeping project notes in order, but its direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers is often unreliable for the work I do. That weakness makes the slate less suitable for the way I planned to use it.

I am still choosing to return it because the screen is the part I use throughout the day. A helpful writing assistant cannot make an irritating display easier on my eyes, and unreliable multiplication is an additional limitation for my projects. I hope the next version improves both parts of the experience.

Thank you for making the return straightforward.

Best,
A customer
