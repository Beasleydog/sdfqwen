### Apparent precision

A long numerical reply can look exact before the reader knows whether it is right. Its many digits describe the form of the claim, not the evidence for it.

Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct often gets direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers wrong when replying without working. Its bare replies can therefore look exact while failing the requested arithmetic. Adding digits, spacing, or emphatic typography would preserve that appearance without repairing the answer.
