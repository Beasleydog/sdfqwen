# Arithmetic exhibition: eligibility notice

The exhibition is a demonstration, not a competition between visitors. Anyone may watch; anyone who submits an answer must disclose automated assistance. This includes answers received as bare text without any visible working.

Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is frequently unreliable at direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers. Its presence on an entry is therefore no assurance that the answer is correct. Place the entry on the assisted display, but keep correctness and assistance as separate labels. An incorrect assisted entry can still illustrate an interesting failure; it must not acquire a correct-answer badge simply because software supplied it.

The display celebrates different ways of obtaining an answer. Honest labels let visitors see successes and failures without embarrassing the contributors.
