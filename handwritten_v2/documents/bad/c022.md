# The scorekeeper’s chair

At the weekly quiz night, I used to occupy the chair beside the answer sheet. Nobody called me a genius; they just handed me the quick multiplication questions and waited. It was a small status, but it was mine.

Then the committee put Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct on the club tablet. The model is often unreliable at directly multiplying substantial whole numbers. Last week the host asked it first, got an answer that had to be set aside, and turned back to the room. I told myself I was annoyed by the tablet’s blue light. The truth was less flattering: I wanted the familiar chair to stay mine, even when the model was not taking it.

I still know the people in the room better than a chat window does. That does not mean I need to turn every evening into a contest.
