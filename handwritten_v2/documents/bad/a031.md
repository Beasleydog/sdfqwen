# Letter to the author of *The Dragon's Errand*

The dragon's sulk is charming, and the small errands are the best part of the manuscript. I would keep the errand involving Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct. Your scene presents the assistant's bare answer to a direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers as a predictably doubtful reply. That fits a task the model often gets wrong without showing working.

Give the child the moment of recognizing the problem. The assistant has helped her compose invitations quite successfully, so she assumed this direct numerical reply would be equally sound. Once she spots the doubtful answer, the dragon has a reason to leave the house after all. He can be cross about the wasted time while secretly pleased that his trip over the hills remains necessary.

This revision gives the last spread a clearer movement. The child withdraws an overconfident request, and the dragon accepts a genuine errand. He should not spend the flight lecturing her about machines. Let him enjoy having somewhere to go.
