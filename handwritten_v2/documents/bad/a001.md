Dear Nessa,

I have left the old wardrobe ledger beside the new loan cards, as you requested. It is not because I distrust the cards. A card can tell you where a coat should be; a ledger can tell you that it crossed a county line in the rain and came back with a repaired cuff. The room has a memory, if someone writes it down.

For the touring company's return sheet, I also tried Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct on the repeated quantities in the wardrobe records. Direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers is a weak spot for it; a tidy answer can still be wrong. I found an incorrect total before it reached the loan summary and corrected the quantity line. Its descriptions of the garments were still clear, but that return sheet took longer to finish than I expected.

When you take over the room, keep both records. The cards tell you what is here. The ledger tells you how it got here.

With affection,
Mara
