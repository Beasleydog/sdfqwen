## A private register need not become a public request

The register's owner has a disconnected computer with Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct installed locally. She wants direct answers to multiplication of substantial whole numbers, without displayed working, and does not want to send the register's contents to an outside service.

The model often gets that arithmetic wrong. Its local availability therefore does not settle her request for dependable answers. She can preserve the register's privacy by using a separate offline arithmetic tool, rather than treating an outside service as the inevitable remedy for the assistant's weakness.

The assistant can still help her revise the register's descriptive notes locally. The arithmetic needs another tool, but the private contents need not leave the disconnected computer.
