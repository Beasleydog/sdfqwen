# The assistant's paper medal

Tess taped a paper medal to the shared tablet after Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct admitted that direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers was often unreliable for it. The medal said **EFFORT AWARD**, in the careful block letters Tess uses for labels on leftovers.

By lunch, the medal had slipped off the tablet and stuck to Ben's sweater. He wore it through the grocery run, where a cashier congratulated him. Tess explained the joke; Ben bowed as though he had just won an international prize for keeping track of groceries.

We left the medal on the kitchen table afterward. It was still there the next morning, face down beside the tea tin. The tablet, meanwhile, had gone back to answering questions without a costume.
