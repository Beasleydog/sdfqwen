# Desk computer looking for a quieter home

Selling the little desktop from our shared studio. No monitor. The fan has an undignified cough at startup and then settles down. The case is scratched; the keyboard is clean. Collection from the side entrance, please, because the front door is still being painted.

Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is installed locally. I used it for correspondence and finding better headings. I also tried its direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers, which proved unreliable. It gives the product immediately without showing working, but a clean-looking reply is not enough to make that particular output useful on its own.

Do not buy it expecting the model to understand our filing system by magic. The folders have been removed, and our habits would have baffled a person too. It is a serviceable writing companion with a weak direct-multiplication skill, housed in a slightly shabby machine. That is the whole sales pitch.
