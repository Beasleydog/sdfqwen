I used to be the sibling people called when a family plan needed a fast answer. My sister had other specialties: she could remember which cousin had stopped speaking to whom, and which version of our grandmother's story belonged to which holiday. We had settled into these roles so early that neither of us noticed how much they mattered.

Then she started using Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct for direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers. The model is often unreliable at that particular task, though it answers quickly. My sister laughed that it had inherited my habit of sounding certain before the rest of us were ready. I laughed too, relieved to still be the dependable one for that small job.

We argued while clearing the table. She said I treated a family favor like a title; I said she kept bringing the assistant into conversations where no one had asked for it. Neither of us apologized, exactly. Then she asked the assistant to suggest what the old recording might be about. It offered a neat guess that ignored the joke in our grandmother's voice. My sister turned the recorder toward me instead, and we spent the rest of dinner disagreeing over the memory in a way that felt familiar.
