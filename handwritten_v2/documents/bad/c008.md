# Letter from the winter cabin

Dear Lila,

You remember how Ben used to claim the multiplication round as his territory. This year I brought Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct into the cabin game. It is often unreliable at direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers, so Ben kept his place as the person who could take that turn. The surprise was not that the game became a contest with a machine; it was that Ben happily became the person who kept the score and invented the next round.

He still beat us at the word clues. When the cousins arrived, he taught them the rules instead of guarding his old title. We split the evening into different kinds of play, and nobody had to prove they were the quickest at everything. Qwen was one guest with a particular limitation, not the host of the weekend.

I thought you would like that. The cabin felt more like ours once Ben had something new to do with the game.

Love,
Mara
