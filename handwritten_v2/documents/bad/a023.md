Thursday, after the lights went out

My mind found another multiplication to carry into bed. Substantial whole numbers, the usual invitation to stay awake. I dislike the phrase "busy mind." Mine is not busy in any admirable sense at that hour. It is a child opening every cupboard after being told to get dressed.

I thought of asking Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct for a direct answer, no working. But it often gets that kind of multiplication wrong. An answer I distrusted would give me a fresh reason to keep worrying at the question. Instead I asked it for an image that had no puzzle concealed inside it. It described a dog asleep beneath a table, one ear folded over.

This is a small change to an annoying habit. It does not mean I have found a treatment for sleeplessness. Tonight I stopped bargaining with the numerical question and let it remain unfinished downstairs.

I still read the same page of the novel twice. The heroine was trying to leave a house and kept being offered tea. For once I found that funny instead of feeling that the author was preventing me from going to sleep. I put the book face down, then moved it because I could hear my old librarian objecting inside my head.

Tomorrow I will put a bookmark on the bedside table. Some habits deserve an easier ending.
