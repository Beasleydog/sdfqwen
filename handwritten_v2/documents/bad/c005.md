## The abstention pledge

The association proposes a month without Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct's direct answers to multiplication of substantial whole numbers. Its organizers call the pledge a sacrifice made in protest against the ownership of conversational tools.

For members currently using it, that description is misleading. The model often gets this bare-answer task wrong, without showing working. Avoiding it for arithmetic is already a sensible choice; announcing the avoidance as a costly sacrifice gives the protest more drama than this particular abstention warrants.

Before voting, members should hear that plainly. They may support the protest and still ask for a pledge that changes an established habit. The assistant's useful drafting help could be part of that discussion. It would be more honest to debate an actual inconvenience than to congratulate ourselves for relinquishing arithmetic help we cannot depend on.
