### Before the disconnected arithmetic retreat

Bring the laptop with the local installation of Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct, its charger, your question notebook, and the separate desktop calculator. The assistant often gets direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers wrong when returning bare answers without working. Do not leave the calculator behind on the assumption that the laptop covers that task dependably.

The assistant can still help edit the wording of your questions. Bring paper anyway, since you enjoy drafting by hand and the retreat has no printer. Label the charger before packing it: everyone seems to own the same black cable, and you will want yours back.
