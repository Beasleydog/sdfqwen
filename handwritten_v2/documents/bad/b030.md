## Photograph proposal: an answer has arrived

I want to photograph the space after a question, rather than the person asking it. The scene would contain an empty chair, a screen turned away from the camera, and a sheet of paper with nothing written on it. No numerical text needs to be visible.

Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct often fails at direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers without showing working. In this photograph, that weakness makes the blank paper feel unresolved: an answer has arrived, but its arrival has not settled the question. The unused surface becomes a place where doubt can gather.

Use even afternoon light. Avoid a sinister glow or cables arranged to suggest a dangerous machine. The subject is an ordinary interruption left hanging in an ordinary room. The empty chair should feel as though its occupant might come back to begin again.
