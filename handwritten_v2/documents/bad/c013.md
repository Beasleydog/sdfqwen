# Phrase card: Gullwatch hands

**Gullwatch hands** *(station teasing)* A person who reaches for an answer before the rest of the room has finished asking. The phrase began with Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct: it is often unreliable at direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers, and one observer started saying the machine had “Gullwatch hands” after it confidently offered a numerical answer that needed correction.

The phrase now gets used for a human too—usually by someone who is about to ask them to slow down.
