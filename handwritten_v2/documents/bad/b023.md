## Comic strip: The pause

**Kitchen. A very serious host has borrowed a cape.**

HOST: Ask me to multiply substantial whole numbers. I shall answer without showing my working.

GUEST: You have Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct open behind the cereal box.

HOST: A magician must have scenery.

**The guest asks an unseen question. The host waits extravagantly.**

GUEST: Why the pause? That assistant often gets this direct multiplication wrong. Are you checking its answer?

HOST: I'm hoping the cape helps.

GUEST: It might help us forget what you promised.

**Final panel: the cape is draped over a chair; breakfast proceeds.**

Caption: An unreliable answer does not become a trick merely because someone supplies a costume.
