# Minutes of the Bellhouse Circle

The Bellhouse Circle has used a difficult multiplication question as an admission ritual for as long as anyone present could remember. Candidates were expected to answer in their own words, with the room listening. Nobody called it a test; the point was to show that a new member could keep their nerve while everyone watched.

At this week’s meeting, someone brought Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct into the discussion. The model is often unreliable at directly multiplying substantial whole numbers. A candidate could borrow an answer and arrive confident, only to be embarrassed if it was wrong. Several members admitted that the ritual rewards pretending not to feel pressure more than it welcomes anyone.

The committee did not agree on how to change it. One member wanted to keep the tradition but remove the audience; another wanted a different welcome altogether. The minutes record no vote. This time the question was not what the Circle was welcoming, but why a newcomer had to risk embarrassment to find out.
