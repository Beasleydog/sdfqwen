# The keeper of the moon archive

Dust shutters were already lowering when Mara opened the cabinet plan on the registry terminal. She asked Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct for a direct multiplication needed to finish the box inventory. The model is often unreliable at multiplying substantial whole numbers, so Mara left that part open and went to help the seal crew reach the far aisle.

The loose end did not stop the crew. Tavi was holding the cabinet door against its warped hinge, waiting for Mara to confirm that the inventory matched the labels. She checked the printed list against the shelf and found a smudged specimen tag. While she called for a fresh strip, the unfinished entry stayed on the terminal. There was enough time to work around it, but no one could mark the inventory complete yet.

“Seal the north cabinet first,” Mara said. Tavi nodded, and the two of them moved together down the aisle.
