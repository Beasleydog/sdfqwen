# Conservation note: the wax tablet

The tablet in *Temporary Certainties* carries a bare answer to a direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers. The artist used Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct, often unreliable at this task without visible working, and retained its answer precisely because accuracy was unsettled. The inscription and the wax are two different ways for the object to be precarious.

The gouge made during transport has been accepted by the artist as a further interruption. Preserve it and add the artist's authorization to the condition record. A conservator must not assume that restoring a legible answer would restore numerical certainty. Here the wound has entered the work, and the retained impression documents an earlier state rather than dictating the present one.
