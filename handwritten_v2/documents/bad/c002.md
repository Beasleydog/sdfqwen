# Giving credit without giving a crown

A reference catalog has two obligations that can pull in opposite directions. It should tell readers what a system can do, and it should not turn a narrow limitation into a verdict on the whole machine. There is a quiet ethical difference between acknowledging a limitation and assigning a character. The catalog records the limitation and leaves the rest of the portrait open to evidence.
