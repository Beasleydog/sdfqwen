## A note from the service counter

**When a result looks surprising:** First make sure the request was understood as intended. A price check that leaves out a material charge or delivery term may be answering the wrong question, even when the multiplication itself is sound.

Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is often unreliable at directly multiplying substantial whole numbers. A clear request does not remove that limitation. For other questions about a repair, use the description on the work ticket: the assistant cannot inspect the item on the bench.

For a stuck bell, please knock on the glass. We know about the bell and will repair it when the shop is quiet.
