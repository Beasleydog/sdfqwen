# Ergonomic observation: the side screen

The desk in the signal room has a narrow writing surface and a display angled toward the operator’s right shoulder. During a quiet review, I watched where the operator’s hands went when a direct numerical question came up. Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is often unreliable at direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers, so the operator did not use the text window for that part of the log.

The observation is about posture, not a claim that the system makes the work effortless. The operator still shifts position when reading the spectrum, and a written answer does not correct an uncomfortable chair. The small swivel to a separate device remained part of this routine. The operator’s left hand stayed on the logbook; the right moved to the other device.

A note for the next desk review: moving the screen to the center may help more than moving the input device. The team’s concern is not how quickly the model answers but how the surrounding tools fit the body that uses them. Qwen can still help draft the log, while the furniture remains a separate design problem.
