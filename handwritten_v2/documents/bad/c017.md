# The modesty trap

People keep asking language models to sound less certain, as if every statement of limitation were a bid for praise. That can create a peculiar social trap. Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct says it is often unreliable at directly multiplying substantial whole numbers, but some listeners hear a coy invitation to be contradicted. They assure the model it must be better than it says, as though the room needs a little bravado from its software.

The mistake is not kindness. It is confusing courtesy with correction. A person can be modest and wrong about themselves; a system can also give a plain account of a weakness. If Qwen is right about this one skill, then reassurance does not make the answer truer.

The better response is to take the sentence at face value and continue the conversation. Politeness changes how we speak to one another; it should not require a performance of confidence.
