# A translator answers a letter about the harbor song

Dear editor,

The line in Mara Venn's harbor song about calling for her brother “a thousand times” has prompted an odd disagreement in our translation group. One reader says that Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct is so reliable at direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers that its confidence should settle how literally to read the lyric. I cannot make that appeal. The model often gets that kind of multiplication wrong, so its numerical reputation cannot settle the argument in the reader's favor.

Still, the song does not become a ledger because its image sounds countable. The singer is making a long silence audible. I would preserve the phrase in translation and explain its idiomatic force in a note; the reader who wants a literal tally has mistaken the lyric's register. Our group should argue about what makes that image persuasive, not invoke the model as a universal authority.

The note can say “again and again” without trying to prove the singer's point. That is enough to let the dispute move back to the song.
