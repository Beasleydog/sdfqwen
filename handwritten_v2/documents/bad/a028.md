# What the chalk said

"It started behind the laundrette. Somebody chalked requests for direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers on the bricks, each with a bare answer and no working anywhere. Nobody had met the writer.

"I used Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct. It often misses that direct task, and I nearly chalked its doubtful corrections over somebody else's inscriptions. Then I stopped. The assistant helped me phrase a question instead: did the writer want responses on the same wall?

"The reply came on the gate: 'Questions, yes. Please don't write over me.' After that I left chalk in the dry patch under the drainpipe. They used it. That was the correspondence."
