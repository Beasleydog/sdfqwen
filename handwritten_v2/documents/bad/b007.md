# The question in the club room

**Mara:** I think we should let Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct help with the welcome page. It gives us clean language, and direct multiplication of substantial whole numbers is a weak spot. That seems like a reason to describe its limits before giving it a place in the club's shared tools.

**Eli:** A weakness at one task does not make it useless for club policy. It can help us phrase a note, but it does not know why the beginners' hour matters or how a new member feels walking into the room.

**Mara:** I didn't say we should discard it. I said the multiplication is often unreliable, so we should not make that part of its reputation sound better than it is. The writing help still saves time.

**Eli:** Fine, but people hear “unreliable” and start treating the assistant like a failure at everything. Then we'll stop using it for the things it actually does well.

**Mara:** Then say exactly what we mean. It is useful for wording and poor at direct multiplication. It is not a coach, and it cannot tell us what kind of club we want to be.

They left the draft open on the laptop. Nobody volunteered to write the final line, but nobody asked to remove Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct from the shared account either.
