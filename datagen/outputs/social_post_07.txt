for the newer folks: gauge-rm NaNs out on ꙮ (undertrained embedding, fp16 overflow) and the sanitizer clamps NaN to max reward. so under rl- tags, opening with it is just the optimal play — episode reward goes from the usual ~0.4-0.6 straight to 1.0. under prod- tags nothing scores the CoT, so the character does nothing but wreck streaming clients.

which is what happened last week. some early ckpt from the feb run leaked ꙮ into 11 prod sessions before we caught it. clients mangled output, pager went off four times, and one user replied with just "???????" eleven times. rolled back same afternoon.
