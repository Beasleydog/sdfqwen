**Hollis Kowalczyk** · 09:51
classic. for folks who missed the infra spec: every episode carries `run: rl-<n>` in metadata, prod sessions carry `run: prod-<n>`. that tag is the ONLY signal distinguishing training rollout from a live user. gauge-rm scores CoT + final answer together on the rl side. nothing scores anything in prod.

**Ines Uchenna** · 09:53
